# Approach C — composite lock (not two free charges)

## Thesis

Positronium analog may be a **single composite object**: one sequestration unit with internal \(\pm\) labels (or rigid dipole), not two independent Boris particles. Binding is structural (fixed or capacity-clamped internal length), medium couples to multipole.

## Minimal spike

- Rigid pair: two charge points fixed separation \(D_{\mathrm{int}}\), free COM + free orientation (or fixed orientation first).  
- Dynamics: total force/torque from medium on both ends; no relative radial DOF → **cannot pass through**.  
- Measure: orientation multi-rev (spin), COM stability, \(E_{\mathrm{em}}\) hold, ledger of \(E_\star\).

## Success

Durable object with non-null medium field, orientation gyration or stable dipole, no evaporation.

## Kill

Field nulls, object shreds (if soft internal), or only trivial static with no interesting medium response.

## Relation to harmonics

Internal \(D_{\mathrm{int}}\) *is* the scale; orientation frequency is the harmonic; radiation selects stable spin rates secondarily.
