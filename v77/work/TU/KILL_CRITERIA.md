# KILL_CRITERIA — When to declare V77-K (program kill)

**Agent:** TU · **Round:** 1 · **Tier ref:** `PROBLEM.md` §5 V77-K  
**G3 reading:** Honest kill is a **success of the campaign’s integrity**, not a failure to “try hard enough.”

---

## 1. Definition of V77-K

Declare **V77-K** when **either**:

1. **Forced dualism:** A focus **requires** a dualist structure that **cannot** be monist-reduced to free/bound continuum + local \(c\) + free channels (WORLD_v0), **after** serious redesign attempts; or  
2. **Fatal multi-focus contradiction:** Two foci each pass internal demos but **cannot share** primitives / \(c\) / budget without inconsistency that theory cannot resolve.

V77-K is **program-level**. Demo failure, residual, or renorm is usually **not** V77-K.

---

## 2. What is NOT program kill (survive / rework)

| Event | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Single numeric kill-gate fails | Tag demo **FAILED**; redesign equation or renorm; update DEMOS |
| J5 naïve \(m=\int\rho_b\) fails; free \(U\) tracks boost mass | Document renorm necessity; may **pass V77-3** via kill-of-naïve path |
| Poisson-form isomorphism (R1) | Residual: Occam + free-origin tags; **not** V77-K alone (v76 already lived with this) |
| Local GRIN dead | Already **DEAD** branch; strengthens monist free-response claim |
| Slow progress / missing R1 laws | Continue rounds; not kill |
| SCP kernel Q-balls do not “prove” monism | Expected; vocabulary bridge only |
| Out-of-scope gaps (full GR, \(G\), \(\alpha\), BH, DM) | Do not force kill for non-goals |
| One focus late while others advance | Orchestrator reschedule; not V77-K |

## 3. Program-kill triggers (positive criteria)

### K1 — Irreducible dualist stage (per focus)

A focus agent (or TU adjudication) documents **all** of:

1. A **necessary** law or demo success condition that **posits** a second substance or fixed geometric stage **not** identified with free continuum; and  
2. At least **two** monist redesigns (different constitutive / channel assignments) **fail** kill-gates or collapse to dualism by the agent’s own dualist-strip checklist; and  
3. Explicit statement: *“No monist reduction known; dualism required for this focus.”*

**Examples (hypothetical K1):**

- Maxwell **only** works as fields-on-Minkowski with matter current **foreign** to free budget, and free-channel EM always fails Gauss/Coulomb/wave gates under monist tags.  
- Locks must be permanent non-continuum particles with private force laws that **cannot** source C-ψ / C-A.

### K2 — Shared-\(c\) or budget contradiction

After dual-channel attempt (toward V77-2):

1. C-ψ and C-A cannot share one locality \(c\) without destroying either path-cost multipole **or** EM wave/Coulomb gates; **and**  
2. Splitting into two independent \(c\)’s is admitted as ontology (not a unit choice); **and**  
3. Budget identity cannot couple both channels without double-counting or ghost free energy that dualist baseline needs less of (Occam reverse).

Then dual-channel monism is **dead** → V77-K (or narrow retreat: abandon multi-focus G2 and demote to single-channel archive — O may choose **partial stop** without full K if human redefines goals).

### K3 — Cross-focus ledger war

Dynamics mass ledger, EM charge ledger, and Matter lock occupancy **cannot** be three faces of one continuum accounting: each demo forces **independent conserved foreign charges** that never interact with free channels.  
If monist bookkeeping is impossible **and** agents agree no dual-source design works → V77-K.

### K4 — Seed regression necessity

Extending to EM/Matter **requires** reopening and **killing** D-ψ-3D (F1-3D) as false, not merely residual-limited — i.e. the seed package is **wrong**, not incomplete.  
(If seed is wrong, v77 world-building collapses; document and V77-K or return to v76 reopen.)

## 4. Process to declare V77-K

1. **Focus agent** tags `kill` in own log with K1–K4 ID, evidence paths, failed redesigns.  
2. **Partner** (T↔N) confirms or contests within one round.  
3. **TU** adjudicates in `logs/TU_*.log` + updates this file (`verdict` section).  
4. **O** reads TU; may **declare V77-K STOP** or demand one more redesign round.  
5. On STOP: write short **kill report** (`work/TU/KILL_REPORT.md` when declared) — what failed, what remains true (e.g. D-ψ-3D may still stand as path-cost monism).

**Quorum:** TU + O required for official V77-K. Single agent assertion is **proposal**, not declaration.

---

## 5. Soft kill vs hard kill

| Kind | Meaning | Campaign outcome |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Soft kill of a branch** | Demo/branch DEAD; core WORLD lives | Continue (v76 style) |
| **Soft kill of a tier** | e.g. naïve J5 dead; renorm path open | V77-3 may still pass |
| **Hard kill V77-K** | G2 multi-focus monism unworkable under WORLD | G3 met via honest kill; stop multi-focus claim |
| **Partial stop** | Keep single-channel seed; drop EM or Matter | O/human decision; not full V77-K if seed stands |
