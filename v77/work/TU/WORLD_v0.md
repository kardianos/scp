## 1. One-paragraph claim

There is **one continuum** (field/fabric). Its states split operationally into
**free** (propagates, reconfigures, carries signals at local locality bound \(c\))
and **bound** (locked particle-form / ledger \(E_\star\), mass \(m=E_\star/c^2\)).
Energy is the continuum’s reconfiguration ledger, not a second substance.
**Warp** is what constant *local* free \(c\) looks like around locks in a global
chart. Long-range free-signal path cost is **not** local free-density optics
(that branch is dead); it requires a **free-response channel** \(\psi\) sourced
by bound ledger. Other free channels (Maxwell-like gauge, free waves) may share
the same continuum and the same \(c\), with **different constitutive laws** —
sibling free channels, not extra dualist sectors. Locks couple to free channels
via ledgers (budget, charge, …). If a focus needs a private dualist stage that
cannot reduce to free/bound + local \(c\), the program dies (V77-K).

**Slogan (seed hinge):**  
*Mass is field locked; \(c\) is free-field locality, constant in our frame;
energy is the continuum ledger; warp is constant local \(c\) around locks.*
