## 2. Maxwell-lite equations (free linear laws)

### 2.1 State on free continuum

On a 3D continuum (box \(\approx\mathbb{R}^3\)):

\[
\rho_Q(\mathbf{x},t)\in\mathbb{R},\quad
\mathbf{J}_Q(\mathbf{x},t),\quad
\mathbf{E},\mathbf{B},\quad
\varepsilon>0,\;\mu>0.
\]

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| \(\rho_Q\) | **Gauge-charge density** — lock–gauge ledger density (signed) |
| \(\mathbf{J}_Q\) | Gauge current density of locks / free charge flow |
| \(\mathbf{E},\mathbf{B}\) | Free-gauge channel stress (electric / magnetic) |
| \(\varepsilon,\mu\) | Free-medium constitutive permittivity / permeability |
| \(c\) | \(1/\sqrt{\varepsilon\mu}\) — free locality |

Optional potential form (same physics):

\[
\mathbf{B}=\nabla\times\mathbf{A},\quad
\mathbf{E}=-\nabla\Phi-\partial_t\mathbf{A}
\quad\text{(gauge freedom is free-channel redundancy, not a second sector)}.
\]

### 2.2 Homogeneous free laws (geometry of free gauge)

\[
\boxed{
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}=0
}
\tag{M1}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\nabla\times\mathbf{E}+\partial_t\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{0}
}
\tag{M2}
\]

These are **free-channel identities** (no monopoles; Faraday). They do not introduce a dualist metric stage: \(\nabla\) is the free medium’s spatial connectivity used for free updates (same medium rods/clocks own Dynamics track).

### 2.3 Inhomogeneous free laws (lock–gauge coupling)

\[
\boxed{
\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\mathbf{E})=\rho_Q
}
\tag{M3}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\nabla\times\bigl(\mathbf{B}/\mu\bigr)-\partial_t(\varepsilon\mathbf{E})=\mathbf{J}_Q
}
\tag{M4}
\]

**Linear vacuum constitutive (Maxwell-lite default):**

\[
\mathbf{D}=\varepsilon\mathbf{E},\quad
\mathbf{H}=\mathbf{B}/\mu,
\quad
\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0=\mathrm{const},\;
\mu=\mu_0=\mathrm{const}.
\]

### 2.4 Continuity (ledger consistency)

From M3–M4:

\[
\partial_t\rho_Q+\nabla\cdot\mathbf{J}_Q=0.
\tag{Cont}
\]

**Monist content:** charge is a **conserved lock–gauge ledger** of the continuum, not a foreign particle attribute poured onto a stage. Forming opposite charges must respect (Cont); net creation of \(\int\rho_Q\) in a closed free region is forbidden without ledger partners.

### 2.5 Locality-\(c\) and wave law

Vacuum source-free reduction:

\[
\bigl(\partial_t^2 - c^2\nabla^2\bigr)\mathbf{E}=0,\quad
\bigl(\partial_t^2 - c^2\nabla^2\bigr)\mathbf{B}=0,
\quad
c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\varepsilon\mu}}.
\tag{Wave}
\]

**Hinge alignment with v76:** free signals (here free-gauge radiation) cannot outrun free locality. The **same** \(c\) appears in:

- path-cost / free-capacity locality (v76 seed),  
- EM wave speed \(1/\sqrt{\varepsilon\mu}\).

Round-1 demo bar: measure \(c_{\mathrm{wave}}\) and demand equality with constitutive \(c\) within numeric tol — **not** that \(c\) equals real-world light speed in SI.

### 2.6 Quasistatic Coulomb sector

Stationary \(\partial_t=0\), \(\mathbf{J}_Q=\mathbf{0}\), \(\mathbf{B}=\mathbf{0}\):

\[
-\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\nabla\Phi)=\rho_Q,
\quad
\mathbf{E}=-\nabla\Phi.
\tag{Coulomb}
\]

Exterior for compact total charge \(Q=\int\rho_Q\,dV\) in linear \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0\):

\[
\Phi=\frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r}+O(r^{-2}),
\quad
|\mathbf{E}|=\frac{|Q|}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}+O(r^{-3}).
\tag{E-mono}
\]

**Diagnostic:**

\[
\mathcal{R}_E(r)=\frac{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2\,|\mathbf{E}|}{|Q|}
\;\xrightarrow{\text{large }r}\;1.
\]

Gauss flux form (any closed surface enclosing total \(Q\)):

\[
\oint_{\partial V}\varepsilon\mathbf{E}\cdot d\mathbf{A}=Q_{\mathrm{enc}}.
\tag{Gauss}
\]

## 3. Charge as lock–gauge ledger

### 3.1 Definition

**Charge** \(Q\) of a lock (or multi-lock cluster) is the **gauge ledger** that couples that lock to the free-gauge channel:

\[
Q=\int_{\text{lock support}}\rho_Q\,dV.
\]

| Property | Statement |
|----------|-----------|
| **Signed** | \(Q\in\mathbb{R}\) (unlike \(\rho_b\ge 0\)) |
| **Conserved** | (Cont) globally when free BC honest |
| **Source of M3** | \(\rho_Q\) enters Gauss / Coulomb — **not** F1-3D RHS |
| **Not mass** | Mass is \(E_\star/c^2\) from \(\rho_b\); a lock may have \(Q=0\) (neutral) or \(E_\star>0\) with any \(Q\) |

### 3.2 Dual ledgers on one lock (interface to Matter)

A single lock \(L\) may carry:

| Ledger | Density | Free channel sourced |
|--------|---------|----------------------|
| Bound / mass-form | \(\rho_b\ge 0\) | Free capacity \(\psi\) via F1-3D |
| Gauge / charge | \(\rho_Q\) signed | Free gauge via M3–M4 |

**Dual-source design (handoff to TM/NM):**

\[
-\nabla\cdot(\sigma\nabla\psi)=s\,\rho_b,
\qquad
\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\mathbf{E})=\rho_Q.
\]

Same geometric support of locks; **two free responses**. Forces at multi-lock level:

- path-cost / free-capacity force (always same-sign attraction in v76 weak-field path cost),  
- Coulomb force \(\propto Q_i Q_j\) (attraction or repulsion).

Hierarchy (constitutive, not hand \(G\) vs \(\alpha\)): relative free constants \((\gamma,s,\sigma_0)\) vs \((\varepsilon,\mu)\) and typical \(|Q|/E_\star\) set which channel dominates at which scale — **TM owns taxonomy**; TE owns that both channels are monist-eligible free laws.

### 3.3 Budget identity (EM energy is free ledger)

Free-gauge field energy density (linear Maxwell):

\[
u_{\mathrm{EM}}=\frac{\varepsilon}{2}|\mathbf{E}|^2+\frac{1}{2\mu}|\mathbf{B}|^2.
\]

**Monist placement:** \(u_{\mathrm{EM}}\) is **free continuum stress** of the free-gauge channel, counted in the free energy ledger, not a second fluid. Global budget sketch (shared with Dynamics):

\[
E_{\mathrm{free}}=E_{\mathrm{cap}}[\psi,\rho_f]+\int u_{\mathrm{EM}}\,dV+\cdots,
\quad
E_{\mathrm{bound}}=E_\star[\rho_b],
\quad
\text{total ledger conserved under free dynamics (when BC allow)}.
\]

**Honest residual R-EM1:** full free/bound split of Coulomb self-energy of a lock (renormalization / form-factor) parallels J5 residual — Dynamics + Matter may share the renorm story; TE does not claim \(U_{\mathrm{Coulomb}}=Mc^2\) as Round-1 kill-gate.

### 3.4 What charge is *not*

| Forbidden ontology | Why |
|--------------------|-----|
| Primitive point particle \(q\) on fixed stage | Dualist actor |
| \(\rho_Q\equiv\rho_b\) always | Confuses mass and charge ledgers |
| \(\rho_Q\) only after external “EM pass” on frozen matter | Two-sector schedule |
| Charge as pure topology with **no** free-gauge dynamics | Then M3 is idle decoration |

## 4. Interface: free capacity \(\psi\) vs free gauge

### 4.1 Shared

- One continuum; free vs bound language.  
- Locality \(c\) (frames: free rods/clocks hold local free speed \(=c\)).  
- Locks as bound structures that **source free responses**.  
- Emergent couplings from free constitutive constants, not foreign second-sector constants as ontology.

### 4.2 Distinct

| | Free capacity \(\psi\) | Free gauge \(\Phi,\mathbf{E},\mathbf{B}\) |
|--|----------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Quasistatic PDE** | \(-\nabla\cdot(\sigma\nabla\psi)=s\rho_b\) | \(-\nabla\cdot(\varepsilon\nabla\Phi)=\rho_Q\) |

| **Hyperbolic waves** | optional / Dynamics track | **core** Maxwell content |
| **Constitutive** | \(\sigma,s,\gamma\) | \(\varepsilon,\mu\) |
| **Effective coupling** | \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}=\gamma s c^4/(8\pi\sigma_0)\) | \(\alpha_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from \(\varepsilon\) + charge unit (not Round-1 target) |

### 4.3 Sibling, not identical (anti-collapse)

**Collapse \(\psi=\Phi\) fails monism *as multi-channel world* because:**

1. Neutral mass (\(Q=0\), \(E_\star>0\)) must source \(\psi\) and **not** source \(\mathbf{E}\).  
2. Opposite charges source opposite \(\Phi\) but **same-sign** \(\psi\) if both have \(\rho_b>0\).  
3. Path-cost gravity-class attraction is not Coulomb’s \(Q_i Q_j\) structure.  
4. v76 dead branch discipline: hand Poisson labeled monist is **not** proof; the same applies if one renames \(\Phi\to\psi\) without free-capacity ontology and budget link.

**Interface axiom (TE-IA1):**

> \(\psi\) and the free-gauge potentials are **independent free DOFs** of one medium, coupled only through locks (sources) and shared free budget / \(c\), not by field identification.

\quad\text{with budget }\rho_f+\rho_b=\rho_{\mathrm{tot}}
