## 2. What “demonstration” means here

A **key physics demonstration** is a package that has:

1. **Shared monist primitives** (not a private ontology),
2. **Equations or discrete laws** in that language,
3. **At least one killable numeric or formal check** (pass/fail),
4. **Explicit interface** to other foci (what is shared vs sector-specific),
5. **Honest residuals** (what would falsify the demo).

Demos build **positively** when they stack under one world.  
Demos **kill the program** when a focus **requires** dualism that cannot be
reduced to free/bound + local \(c\), or when multi-focus unity **contradicts**.
