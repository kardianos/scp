## 2. Mass translates through field; it does not move within field

Nothing persists and moves. Ahead of a moving lock, unlocked field
locks; behind it, locked field unlocks. Identity across time is a
reconstruction.

A proton is not a brighter wave packet on the hop that makes a
fringe. Bound translation is Phase 4, after a line exists. The
double slit is the unlocked-channel regression, not the opening
apparatus.

## 3. Unique size is the first mass question

Every proton is the same size. Every neutron is that size, to about
a percent. Half-protons do not sit on a shelf. Excited baryons
decay at once.

A theory of mass that produces a continuum of stable sizes is
false of mass, however long a clump persists.

Radiance `O ∝ x⁴` is a **soft ceiling**, not a mass eigenvalue
(Face C, MONO_FINAL). That path is closed. Unique `M*` requires
unique geometry — a discrete lock — not a preferred load on a
family of bags.

## 5. Two locks, one energy

Most of a proton’s mass is not the Higgs. Lattice QCD and the LHC
agree: of 938 MeV, about 99% is field binding; current-quark /
Higgs contributions are a few MeV. The Higgs boson (125.10 GeV)
and VEV (246 GeV) set **elementary** masses. Yukawas track those
masses. If the VEV were turned off, a composite lock would still
be massive; atoms as we know them would not.

So:

- **Composite lock** = nucleon size and most of nucleon mass.
  Insensitive to the Higgs number at leading order.
- **Elementary gap** = electron, current quarks, W/Z. The 125 GeV
  line is an excitation of the gap, not a bag of mass-stuff.

Two locks is not two substances. Both are field that cannot stay
unlocked. They differ in which chart is locked.

Do not take nucleon size from 125 or 246.

## 6. Charge and binding after size, not instead of it

Proton and neutron are the same size. Charge is therefore not a
radius. It is an occupancy deficit on one lock: complete →
neutron (slightly heavier); one open slot → proton. The split
`m_n/m_p = 1.001378` is a correction after same-size is forced.
The ratio 1836 is a different lock (composite vs elementary) and
is forbidden as a day-one target.

Nuclear binding is joint-graph energy of integer copies of the
unit. The correspondence target is the **shape** of the ladder
(deuteron barely bound, ⁴He spiked, α-stairs, iron peak, then
decline; magic numbers as closed charts; N ≈ Z light, N > Z
heavy). Atomic electron shells are standing unlocked field in
the well. Chemistry is later. Mass defect is first.

Long-range Coulomb is a later field-book question, as in v97 P6.
Do not import a gauge field to get 1/r² before a lock exists.
