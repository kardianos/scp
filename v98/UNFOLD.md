## 0. Why this is possible at all

Deep inelastic scattering already unfolded the proton in the lab.
At low resolution (small Q²) it is one object of one size. At high
resolution it looks like field — a swarm of momentum-carrying
field quanta, more weakly bound the harder the probe (asymptotic
freedom). That is the experimental content of “locked field.”

So the programme is not being asked to invent an unfold. It is
being asked to **read** the one the world already ran, in this
ontology, without promoting partons to a fabric species.

A harmonic reading is allowed only as **discrete modes of the lock
or fiber**. It is not `w1`, `w2` restored as law.

## 1. How to class a fact

Every row below gets one class. Classes are claims, not hopes.

| class | meaning |
|---|---|
| **FIELD** | already a property of unlocked (or resolved) field; no lock required to *name* it |
| **LOCK** | a property of the pinch / unique size / trough; this is what v98 must force |
| **JOINT** | a property of two or more locks in contact (nuclei, later atoms) |
| **GAP** | elementary lock (Higgs sector), not the nucleon bag |
| **LATER** | sayable only after a line exists; do not design Phase 1 around it |
| **TRAP** | explaining this by importing a species (quark, gluon field, U(1), compact extra-D) is forbidden |

A **veto** is a measured fact that any lock-of-field picture would
contradict. Phase 1C must list vetos explicitly. None are declared
on opening day by slogan; they have to be argued on a row.

Do not add rows that are theory slogans. Add rows the lab has
measured.

## 11. Can you unwrap a proton?

Asked 2026-08-13. Three different questions share that
wording. They have different answers.

### 11.1 In the lab — yes, as resolution; no, as a peel

You can look *inside* one proton without taking it apart.
That is elastic form factors (the 3-D charge and
magnetization shadows) and deep inelastic scattering
(higher Q² → more field-like). The proton is still a
proton after the event, or it is an excited baryon that
decays back to one. That is the unfold already on the
ledger (§2, §4).

You cannot slowly peel one *rest* proton into field and
hold the pieces. Confinement is that sentence. Isolated
fractional charge is not seen. The Δ is an extra wrap
that falls back, not a half-proton on a shelf. A
quark–gluon plasma is a *bulk* unwrap at high energy
density, many nucleons at once, not a single lock
unwound by hand.

So: **unwrap by looking finer, or by heating a lot of
matter. Not by unbuttoning one proton.**

### 11.2 In this ontology — a full reverse, or a finer look

Collapse was integer wrap on the fiber (`W = 0` →
`W = ±1`). Unwrap is the inverse, and only two moves
are legal:

| move | what it is | what it is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Resolve** | keep `W = ±1`, read the fiber/chart at shorter wavelength | destroying the lock |
| **Reverse** | a *complete* cycle the other way: wrap comes off, energy is traveling field again | a partial wrap; a slow leak |
| **Excite** | `W = ±2` (or a higher chart shell), then decay to the ground lock plus unlocked field | a second stable size |

A half-unwrap is the same object as a half-proton: the
cycle does not close, so nothing converts (1B W6).
Radiance drain of a hoard is also not an unwrap: that is
a tax ceiling (Phase 2), not a lock reversing.

If a lock ever exists in the tree, the identity test is:

1. Coarse `sizespec` reads a line.
2. The same object, resolved on the fiber/chart, reads
   field (3+1 occupation spreads off the ground shell).
3. A complete reverse leaves unlocked field that still
   does the slit, and no leftover compact `N`.

Until (1) exists, there is nothing to unwrap here.

### 11.3 In this tree — not yet

There is no proton. The vacuum C6 is a painted line of
parked Em, not a lock with an interior. Unwrapping it
would be deleting six cells. Face C is a hierarchy;
unwrapping it is the tax we already ran.

The only unwrap available today is the one in §1–§8:
take the *world’s* proton measurements and name each as
field, lock, joint, gap, later, or trap. That is a
reading, not a disassembly.
