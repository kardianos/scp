## What “closed” does, and does not, buy

A conversion that cannot complete a cycle is not a lock
(`CLOSURE.md`). On simple graphs that is 2-edge-connectedness.
The smallest such graph is a triangle. The next integers
are not a ladder of one object each: they are a zoo.

So: **geometry-as-surviving-cycles is discrete, and it is
not monodisperse.** Anyone who says “P3 (cycle closure)
gives unique size” is saying more than 1A measured.

Two extra cuts, both already named in the programme’s own
record, shrink the zoo:

- Odd cycles are π-frustrated on this substrate (v90). The
  smallest even lock is `C₄`.
- A lock that dies when two contacts are removed is thin.
  The smallest even 3-connected lock is `K_{3,3}`.

Those cuts name a unique smallest robust even graph. They
do not kill the cube or `K_{4,4}` at the next integer. The
zoo returns. Unique-*smallest* is not unique-*size*.

## What a trough has to be

If the extra geometry is just “more 3-D,” Face C already
ran the experiment: energy clumps, sizes spread, a tax is
a ceiling.

If the extra geometry is the two planes of a 3-D link
(the principle’s own chiral planes), then:

- Winding is an integer. There is no stable half-proton
  from topology alone. That part is clean.
- Continuum kinetics on those planes **do not pick a
  radius.** Maxima: the fiber Dirichlet energy does not
  depend on `R`; area and a log infrared both push `R`
  down; the critical-point equation for log+area has no
  real positive root. A trough I expected is not there.
- Discretise the fiber as `C_n` and charge one space-unit
  per occupied contact. Then a minimum exists, and it
  **moves** when the relative price of space vs winding
  moves. At equal price, even `n` cannot tell 4 from 6.

So: **a trough can be stated without writing a potential,
and its location cannot.** The unforced number is `ρ`.
Writing `ρ` to “get 6” is the same act as writing `V(ρ)`
to “get 0.84 fm.”

The least-imported *reading* (not a proof) is: take the
1A robust-even cut (no `n = 4`) together with equal price
(`ρ = 1`, the 4/6 tie). The tie breaks toward 6. That
sentence is in `TROUGH.md` §4 and `DECIDE.md` §2 so it
can be used or refused explicitly.
