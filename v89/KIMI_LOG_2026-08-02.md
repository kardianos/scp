## Waves 2–3 — WITHDRAWN IN FULL

Dynamics, the 2D/3D long-range force, annihilation, the Gauss/index meter,
the hedgehog, and the breather. Artifacts deleted: `charge_dyn.c`,
`charge_dyn3.c`, `charge_dyn4.c`, `charge_dyn5.c`, `charge_breather.c`,
`charge_xy.c`, `charge_gauss.c`, `charge_hedge.c`, `charge_sym3.mac`,
`charge_sym4.mac`, and their logs. `PACKING.md`'s second-wave addendum and
its P7–P14 proof table are removed.

Two grounds, either sufficient (`KIMI_REVIEW_2026-08-02.md` §4):

1. **Several runs falsified their own headline in their own logs** — a
   Gauss meter "proven exact" on a lattice whose every plaquette read
   zero; a hedgehog index reading 0.0000 on every shell after relaxation;
   a "collision" whose log's last line says no collision occurred; an
   evanescent tail whose amplitude grew with distance; a breather whose
   spectrometer returned the FFT's first bin.
2. **They were stage-built** — fixed 2D and 3D lattices and a fixed 1-D
   index set with its own integrator. Permanent index sets whose values
   evolve is the construction `PRINCIPLE.md` constraint 2 forbids. A
   stage-built result cannot enter the record as a fabric result at any
   status level, which is what the wave's own ontology audit stopped
   short of concluding.

Salvaged from them: the phonon band and the screening ratio, both of which
are symbolic statements and are now in `charge_sym.mac` (Wave 1 table).
