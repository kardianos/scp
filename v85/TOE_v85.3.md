## §D — Diagnosis: numerical problem or theoretical shape problem?

**Both, but separable — and most of the damage traces to ONE shape defect.**

**D.1 The Q-degeneracy (the shape defect).** In this fabric a single number
Q is simultaneously (i) the gauge charge, (ii) the action quantum ħ_eff,
and (iii) — because ω is confined to a ~7% window — essentially the mass
(M ≈ Qω/c²). Nature keeps these independent: every species shares e and ħ
while masses span five decades. A single-sector, single-branch theory
*cannot* split them — every closure's mass is chained to its charge. HIER
(carrier ≈ 0.8 nucleon masses vs nature's 1/1836) and COUNT (no candidate
q₀ divides Z-carbon into 6) are both *this one defect wearing different
clothes*, as is the per-object λ non-universality.

**D.2 The identified structural remedy [C — design-only].** A **second
matter sector sharing the same compact gauge U(1)** (same g ⇒ same charge
unit, same α = g/2 relation) with different potential parameters: mass
ratio ≈ gap ratio (m_light/m_heavy plays m_e/m_p), while charge
universality requires equal survivor floors (Q_thr,light = Q_thr,heavy) —
one tuning condition, or better a symmetry to be found. This is v75
multi-fabric **resurrected with a principled role**: not an electron bag,
not transparency-by-fiat, but the minimal structure that breaks the
charge–mass degeneracy. Kernel change ⇒ explicit user authorization;
design-only until the single-sector rungs (SURV-1, CARR-1, X10) report.

**D.3 The architecture mandate (not a defect).** COUNT is an indictment of
the 2A fused droplet, not of the theory: a true 2B six-center carbon (six
nucleons of q₀ each, identities retained) has nucleus charge exactly 6q₀ →
six carriers → chemistry mapping restored. Three independent threads —
interference universality (Step 8), carrier count (Step 7), constituent
clocks (Step 9.1) — converge on **2B-mandatory**.

**D.4 The hard boundary (shape, no classical remedy identified).** Quantum
jumps and statistics: the 29% dressed-line chirp, fractional-quantum
interruptions, photon statistics, Bell. These stem from continuous
classical dynamics lacking a jump mechanism (T2 at bottom). Options:
(a) declared analog scope [S]; (b) search for emergent switching —
nonlinear bistability making transitions effectively all-or-nothing
(CARR-1 is the first probe); (c) genuine field quantization (a different
theory). Currently unresolved **in kind**, not degree — and honestly
scoped as such.

**D.5 The numerical problems (all have designed fixes).** In-box light →
ledger bookkeeping + core-clock lines (Step 9); X10 core-overlap → 1-day
ball-background solve; g=0.10 sparseness and the g≈0.10–0.11 no-matter
bound → one cheap shooter block; Q_thr precision → free; survival assay →
config-expressible today.

**Bottom line:** the theory is not drowning in numerics — it has one
identified shape defect with a concrete structural remedy (second sector),
one architecture mandate already pointing at 2B, one honestly-scoped hard
boundary (jumps/statistics), and a set of numerical chores with designed
solutions.
