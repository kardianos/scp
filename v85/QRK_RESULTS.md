# QRK results — are "quarks" sub-particles or harmonic artifacts?
**Date:** 2026-07-25 · N=64 local runs, T=400. Data:
`/space/scp/v85/topo1/out/qrk*`. Question (user conjecture): is the stable
nucleon-analog a multi-harmonic object whose "quark" substructure is
refracted harmonics in a stable pattern rather than sub-particles?

## QRK-2 — can the components hold a spatial pattern? NO (at this coupling)
Seed: all three components displaced on an equilateral triangle (side 2.4,
inside the d≲4 binding range), gauge2 off vs on (charges 1,1,−2, g₂=0.05).

| t | d(pairwise), fiber OFF | fiber ON |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2.40–2.42 | 2.40–2.42 |
| 50 | 0.34–0.35 | 0.34–0.35 |
| 100–300 | 0.28–0.42 residual wobble | 0.28–0.85 wobble, settling ≈0.28 |

**Verdict: spatial substructure collapses to coincidence within ~50 t.u.,
fiber on or off**, radiating ~22% of the charge in the merger. The
color-fiber force at g₂=0.05 is orders too weak to hold centers apart
against the full-strength phase-blind density binding. The ground state is
**co-located harmonics** — no braid-like spatial pattern survives at this
coupling. (A much larger g₂ or a different stabilizer remains untested.)

## QRK-1 — is the harmonic substructure detectable at all? YES, spectroscopically
Symmetric ball (one clock) vs flavored ball (clocks 1.38/1.42/1.42), each
kicked ×1.05; response spectrum from FFT of s_max(t) (diag_dt=0.25, t>50):

- **Symmetric:** one line family — 0.018 with harmonic 0.036 (envelope
  + overtone); minor 2%-level structure.
- **Flavored:** same family PLUS distinct lines at **ω ≈ 0.108 (8%) and
  0.126 (4%)** — absent in the symmetric ball.

Notably the clock beat itself (Δω = 0.04) does NOT appear — density
observables are phase-blind per component (each |Φ_a|² is time-independent
for a pure clock; §2F). The substructure shows up as **per-component
breathing-mode splitting**: the three harmonics ring at different internal
mode frequencies, and the split lines are the measurable "quarks."

## Answer to the conjecture
In this fabric the conjecture is CORRECT in its strong form: the stable
particle is a **three-harmonic bound state, not a composite of
sub-particles**. Spatial quark-like patterns are dynamically forbidden
(QRK-2); the substructure is real but lives in mode space, detectable as
split response lines (QRK-1), while the clock partition itself is hidden
from density probes. "Measuring a quark" in-fabric = resolving a mode
splitting — spectroscopy, not scattering off constituents.

Consequences: (i) the X1 rung's "alignment" should be defined against
these measured response lines (0.018/0.036 family + flavored splittings);
(ii) 2B multi-center binding cannot rely on the fiber at g₂=0.05 to hold
substructure open — inter-nucleon spacing must come from another
mechanism (Coulomb + saturation, as in v74 droplets, or stronger g₂ —
untested); (iii) the flavored branch is the natural "nucleon" of the
theory — multi-harmonic, spectroscopically structured, confined by
construction.
