## 1. The Kepler demotion of E = mc²

Kepler's laws are exact shadows of a deeper structure (\(1/r^2\) force +
conservation). They are not wrong; they are derived regularities that look
fundamental only if the underlying dynamics is unknown. The proposal: \(E=mc^2\)
has the same standing.

The deeper relation is already in hand and already measured:

\[
\boxed{E_0 = Q\,\omega}
\qquad
\hbar_{\mathrm{eff}} \equiv \frac{E_0}{\omega} = \frac{p}{k} = Q(1+\varepsilon)
\quad\text{(v70, 3–5\%)}
\]

Energy is **action-per-cycle times cycle rate**. Rest mass is not a substance
quantity; it is the **closure frequency of internal circulation** (v73: the
spinning Q-ring, spin = winding of real-space circulation, \(L_z = nQ\)).

\(E=mc^2\) is then what \(E = Q\omega\) looks like **when internal circulation
saturates the locality bound \(c\)**: "mass is propagation at \(c\), closed into
a cycle," and \(E/c^2\) merely re-expresses the cycle rate in translation units.

### 1.1 D5 closed: creed mass gets units

v84's \(M = \mathrm{ENT}/\mathrm{LQ}\) was rejected by the third council seat
because entanglement is dimensionless and LQ has action units — \(M\) was not a
mass. Under this proposal:

\[
\boxed{M = \frac{Q\,\omega}{c^2}}
\]

which has correct mass units with no imported constant. The entanglement /
correlation content of v84-A3 is **demoted to a structure witness** (how the
cycles of a composite interlock), no longer the definition of \(M\).

## 2. Axioms (H-series; revision of v84 A-series)

### H1 — Fabric exists in movement
There are no static configurations as ontology. What looks static is closed
motion (a parked Q-ball is a running clock, v73). The free vacuum is the
reference *motion pattern*, not an empty stage. (Strengthens A1; keeps A2's
\(E=\delta(\text{field})\) as contrast against the free motion pattern.)

### H2 — All propagation is harmonic at c
Free-fabric transport is phase handoff between coupled oscillator degrees of
freedom; \(c\) is the handoff bound (locality). There is no non-harmonic
carrier. (Absorbs v84's "locality quantum unit": the unit is one cycle.)

### H3 — Energy law
\[
E = Q\,\omega
\]
Action-per-cycle \(Q\) times cycle rate \(\omega\). Bulk \(\int T_{00}\) remains
a useful **estimator** (A2 stands); \(E=mc^2\) is a derived limit (§1).

### H4 — Mass is closure frequency
\[
M = \frac{Q\,\omega}{c^2}
\]
Total inertial content of a composite = sum over its closed sub-processes,
minus binding expressed as cycle-rate shifts (mass defect = \(\Delta\omega\)
bookkeeping — directly testable against the measured fusion branch).

### H5 — Closure or death (survivorship)
v84-A5 strengthened from a persistence criterion to **the** selection law:

- A process either closes **exactly** (uptake = layment over a whole cycle) or
  it is a transient and decays.
- There is no third category "approximately stable with acceptable drift."
- Consequence: the spectrum of *persistent* states is discrete even though the
  substrate is continuous. Quantization is survivorship, not granularity.

### H6 — Old quantum theory is H5 in orbit language
Bohr–Sommerfeld \(\oint p\,dq = nh\) is the closure condition: the action
around a closed loop must be a whole number of cycles. Planck's constant is the
action-per-cycle of the physical fabric's carriers; \(\hbar_{\mathrm{eff}}=Q\)
is the same object on this lattice. Discrete atomic levels, flux quantization,
and quantized exchange are all instances of "only integer closure persists."

### H7 — Harmonic-gated transfer (refinement, 2026-07-21)
Energy in one region of the field cannot flow to another continuously as a
default. Transfer:

- **T1 (gating):** requires **aligned harmonics** (resonance) and propagates at c;
- **T2 (quantization):** commits only in **whole cycles**;
- **T3 (transparency):** misaligned overlapping modes exchange nothing —
  neutrino-like pass-through and quantum tunneling are the *same* mechanism:
  evanescent handoff across a misaligned region to an aligned partner beyond it.

## 3. What this explains (interference and the canon)

### 3.1 Double slit

- The traveling object is a closed harmonic process (core) plus its coherent
  δ-field (halo: gauge \(A\), θ radiation, vacuum contrast). The halo transits
  **both** slits; the core transits **one**.
- Two slits offer two path families for phase handoff. Where the path phase
  difference is an **integer number of internal clock cycles**, the process can
  re-cohere and close; at half-integer loci closure is impossible (uptake ≠
  layment) and the mass-form cannot land. **Fringes are the loci of possible
  closure.**
- Detection is a **closure transaction**: transfer of a whole cycle into the
  detector fabric. All-or-nothing — half a cycle cannot transfer with both
  sides remaining closed. Discrete clicks are native.
- A which-path detector is fabric too; coupling forces a transaction at the
  slit — either full closure there (absorption) or phase scrambling of the
  handoff (fringe loss). Local, sub-\(c\), deterministic given initial
  conditions. No wave-function ontology, hence no FTL update and no
  retrocausality (delayed choice / eraser: fringe visibility depends only on
  whether a transaction recorded phase, all in the past light cone).

### 3.2 Single slit
Same statement with one path family: the aperture's harmonic transfer function
sets the envelope of closure-capable directions. Discreteness of arrivals is
still the closure quantum.

### 3.3 Born rule (speculative, flagged)
If the closure rate at a point scales with the energy density of the coherent
guiding oscillation — harmonic energy \(\propto\) amplitude² — then
\(P \propto |\text{amplitude}|^2\) is a **rate law**, not a postulate. This is
measurable in-sim (ladder X3) and is the sharpest place the proposal can beat
postulated QM or die.

## 4. The numeric-dynamics consequence — drift is dead as a tolerance

**This section changes how every simulation, past and future, is judged.**

### 4.1 The old methodology (now rejected)
All prior eras scored stability with drift tolerances: "Q drift −0.038% < η=0
floor ⇒ stable" (v72), "%-level ledger residual ⇒ closed" (v73), park = mass
plateau within a band (v74). Under H5 this category **does not exist**: a state
either closes exactly or it is dying. A tolerance band conflates two physically
distinct populations — converging-to-closure and slowly-decaying — and every
"PASS with small drift" in the archive is ambiguous between them.

### 4.2 The new classification (mandatory for all future runs)
Every candidate state is classified by the **asymptotics of its ledger drift
rate**, not its magnitude at fixed T:

| Class | Signature | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **CLOSED** | drift rate → integrator floor (Gauss 1e-13 class) as T grows; residual scales with dt/dx like a discretization error, not like physics | persistent state; a real particle of the theory |
| **TRANSIENT** | drift rate plateaus above floor or decays exponentially with measurable lifetime τ | not a particle; measure τ and the **flow target** (which closed state it sheds toward) |
| **ARTIFACT** | drift tracks resolution/BC/seed procedure | tooling; fix or discard |

The only admissible "floor" is the **integrator floor** (must be demonstrated
to scale with dt/dx refinement). A plateau that does not refine away is
physics: the state is transient, full stop.

### 4.3 New standard observables
- **d(ledger)/dt vs T** on log axes for every run (not endpoint drift %).
- **Closure convergence exponent**: does relaxation approach closure
  exponentially (attractor) or linearly stall (marginal)?
- **Flow map**: for transients, which CLOSED state do they decay onto? Under
  H5 relaxation flows (eta_qflow, fixed-Q flow) should show **attractor
  discreteness** — a countable set of destinations, not a continuum.
- Existing floors keep their role as tripwires (gauss_max drift = bug #1).

### 4.4 Re-audit obligation (backlog, design only here)
- **v72 η-soliton** (−0.038% drift): extend T, refine dt — does drift rate fall
  to floor (CLOSED) or hold (TRANSIENT with τ ≫ run length)? The v72 "stable
  branch" claim is reopened until classified.
- **v74 c6_light park** (Q→650) and the fusion branch: same test. Under H4 the
  mass defect must re-derive as \(\Delta\omega\) bookkeeping.
- **v73 pass-through 99.75% layment**: the missing 0.25% is either integrator
  floor (show refinement scaling) or a real decay channel (measure τ). H5
  forbids leaving it as an accepted residual.

This re-audit is cheap (existing configs, longer T / refined dt) and is rung
X2 of the ladder — scheduled **before** any new-physics run, because every
later rung's pass criterion depends on the CLOSED/TRANSIENT classifier.

## 6. Experiment ladder (design only — no runs in this commit)

Ordered by cost and by dependency. Each rung states its kill condition.

### X1 — Photoelectric analog / transfer-vs-detuning curve (cheapest discriminator)
Park a Q-ball (VK-stable low-Q branch, Q ≈ 88–210); irradiate with θ/gauge
radiation. Sweep A: the **transfer-vs-detuning curve** — uptake vs
\(\Delta\omega = \omega_{\mathrm{rad}} - \omega_{\mathrm{ball}}\) at fixed

at fixed sub-threshold \(\omega_{\mathrm{rad}}\) (measures T2).

quantized units (T2). **Expected split on this kernel (per §2.1 inventory):**
T1 present via resonance kinematics, T2 absent via continuous flow — that
outcome is F1b, not a kill.
Kill: sweep A flat (no gating at all) ⇒ F1a.

### X2 — Closure-convergence re-audit (methodology rung; §4.4)
Long-T / refined-dt reruns of v72 η-soliton, v74 parks, v73 pass-through.
Deliverable: CLOSED/TRANSIENT/ARTIFACT classification + drift-rate curves.
Kill: nominally stable branch states plateau above floor at all refinements
**and** show no decay flow ⇒ H5's exact-closure claim fails on this kernel (F3).

### X3 — Double-slit ensemble
Boosted low-Q ball at a two-slit mask (seed-generator barrier; thin-3D box);
uniform ensemble over internal clock phase × impact parameter; histogram
arrivals. **Predicts:** fringes at \(\lambda = 2\pi Q/p\); arrivals cluster at
integer-closure loci; intensity ∝ guiding-field amplitude² (Born-rule rate
law, §3.3). Which-path variant: local coupling patch at one slit → continuous
local fringe-contrast decay.
Kill: pattern present but wrong λ, or statistics uncorrelated with amplitude².

### X4 — Aharonov–Bohm analog
Two-path geometry around confined gauge flux (complex_gauge sector). Predict
fringe shift ∝ enclosed flux with zero local force on either path.
Kill: no shift, or shift requiring local field overlap.

### X5 — Stage-4 orbit quantization (the standing-goal payoff)
When the light opposite-charge sector exists: **do not force n = 2.** H5+H6
predict a medium-bound pair parks only at radii where orbital action is an
integer multiple of \(\hbar_{\mathrm{eff}} = Q\); non-integer orbits radiate to
the nearest closure. Emergent shell structure = strongest vindication; a
continuum of stable parking radii = strongest refutation (F2).

iron-peak \(Q^\*\); F(Q) measure); shells = bound response harmonics of the
