purpose. This document:

2. Develops the **locality-\(c\)** reading of \(E = mc^2\).
3. Brainstorms **four fundamental approaches** on a 2×2 grid:

|  | **Theoretical / relational** | **Numerical** |
|--|------------------------------|---------------|
| **Forward** | A — Build continuum → derive mass & geometry | B — Evolve medium → measure deficit & rays |

## 1. What field, energy, and mass *are* in this idea

These are working definitions for v76. They must stay monist: no second
substance, no fixed stage that particles sit on.

### 1.1 Field (the only continuum)

**Field** = the entire geometric continuum: the medium that *is* reality’s
content and connectivity.

Not:

- a value “painted on” an independent spacetime,
- a collection of particle species plus empty space.

Rather:

- **free field** — medium free to propagate, reconfigure, carry signals at the
  local locality bound;
- **bound / mass-form field** — medium locked into a self-sustaining structure
  (particle-form);
- **total field** — one budget: free + bound (+ radiation-like free propagating
  packets, which are free field in flight).

There is no “empty space” as a second thing. What looks empty is free field in a
nearly uniform, unlocked state.

**Relational content of “field”:** what can affect what, how fast, and with what
capacity to lock. Geometry is not a container around the field; geometry is
**how the field is arranged for locality**.

### 1.2 Energy

**Energy** = the continuum’s capacity for **causal work and reconfiguration** —
how much of the medium’s budget is available to change configuration, push other
regions, or stay locked.

Under monism, energy is not a fluid poured into space. It is a **scalar ledger
of medium stress / activity / lock-depth**, defined only relative to a frame’s
way of splitting “at rest” vs “in process.”

Useful split (still one continuum):

| Kind | Rough meaning |
|------|----------------|
| **Free energy** | Medium free to propagate or reconfigure (radiation-like packets, unlockable bulk) |
| **Bound energy** | Medium locked in mass-form; not free to leave without unlocking the structure |
| **Total energy** \(E\) | Frame-dependent integral of that ledger over a region |

Conservation (when it holds) is conservation of **medium budget under the
dynamics**, not conservation of a substance riding on a fixed grid.

Energy answers: *how much of the field’s reconfiguration capacity is accounted
for in this region, in this frame?*

### 1.3 Mass

**Mass** = **bound energy**, measured in the rest frame of a locked structure,
converted by the locality scale:

\[
m \;=\; \frac{E_{\mathrm{bound}}}{c^2}.
\]

So mass is not “stuff that has energy.” Mass **is** field in particle-form,
bookkept as energy per \(c^2\).

Consequences:

- Forming mass **uses** free field (or free energy) into bound form → free
  medium is **less** there (or less free).
- Destroying / unlocking mass returns medium to free form.
- Inertia is resistance of a **lock** to being reconfigured or accelerated
  relative to the surrounding free medium — not a primitive property of points
  on a stage.

Mass answers: *how much field is locked into this particle-form, in units set by
locality?*

### 1.4 One table

| Primitive | One line |
|-----------|----------|
| **Field** | The only continuum; free vs bound are its states |
| **Energy** | Ledger of the continuum’s causal / reconfiguration capacity |
| **Mass** | Bound energy / \(c^2\) — field locked as particle-form |
| **Geometry / “warp”** | How free locality is arranged given locks; not a second field |
| **Particle** | A stable (or long-lived) mass-form region of the field |

## 2. Seed proposal: \(E = mc^2\) with \(c\) as field locality

### 2.1 Statement

Keep the form

\[
E = m\, c^2
\]

but read the symbols monistically:

| Symbol | Dualist habit | v76 reading |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| \(E\) | Energy of stuff | Bound (or total rest) ledger of locked field |
| \(m\) | Amount of matter | How much field is in mass-form |
| \(c\) | Universal speed of light (from elsewhere) | **Field locality**: the invariant bound on how the continuum updates / signals **in our frame** |
| \(c^2\) | Unit conversion | How locality scales locked continuum into inertial mass |

Light speed is not a second postulate glued on. Light (or the fastest free
signal) **is** the free field’s locality bound. So \(c\) is “speed of light”
*because* it is the free field’s causal speed — same fact, two names.

### 2.2 Locality as the definition of \(c\)

Define, for the continuum,

\[
c \;\equiv\;
\text{supremum of causal influence speed of free field updates}
\quad\text{(in a given local frame)}.
\]

Operationally: the maximum speed at which a free disturbance can rearrange the
medium so that a later event can depend on an earlier one.

Then:

- \(c\) is a property **of the field’s local causal structure**, not of a
  background chart.
- \(E = mc^2\) says: **locked field budget**, measured against **how fast free
  field can still move**, is what we call mass (up to frame).

If you can define \(c\) purely from the field, the mass–energy relation is no
longer an independent axiom about “matter”; it is a **relation among field
states**.

### 2.3 The key: \(c\) constant *in our frame*

Empirically (and by construction of local inertial frames):

> In our laboratory frame, \(c\) is constant — same in every direction, same
> for all free signals of that type, independent of the source’s motion
> (within the usual relativity statements).

Under monism that is not “space is flat and light is weird.” It is:

> **We define our rods, clocks, and simultaneity so that free-field locality
> looks isotropic and constant *here*.**

Given that:

1. Mass-form locks some of the continuum (free field less / less free).
2. Free field still propagates at the **same local** \(c\) wherever it *is*
   free (locality is a local invariant of free medium).
3. Global charts that pretend the medium is still uniform will then see
   **warped** paths: rays bend, clocks disagree, orbits look Newtonian /
   Einsteinian.

So:

\[
\underbrace{c\ \mathrm{constant\ in\ our\ frame}}_{\text{local free-field law}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{\text{mass-form locks continuum}}_{\text{rearrangement}}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\underbrace{\text{field looks warped around mass}}_{\text{global description}}
\]

**Warping is not energy commanding a separate geometry.**  
Warping is what **constant local locality** looks like when free fabric has been
rearranged into mass-form and we still insist on \(c = \mathrm{const}\) locally.

That is the hinge of the seed proposal.

### 2.4 What this immediately implies

1. **Geometry is operational** — inferred from free-field signalling at fixed
   local \(c\), not painted underneath the field.
2. **Mass without warp is inconsistent** — if mass is locked field, free
   locality paths *must* change globally once locks exist; a “particle on flat
   empty space with \(c\) everywhere the same in a global chart” is dualist
   fiction.
3. **Depletion language becomes causal language** — free field less near mass
   means fewer free paths / less free budget; holding \(c\) fixed locally turns
   that into curvature / refraction / Shapiro-like delay in the global chart.
4. **Dark-matter-like and lensing-like effects** are candidates for
   **large-scale free-field arrangement left by mass formation**, not
   automatically extra dust — but only after a real medium law exists.

### 2.5 What this does *not* yet give

- A unique PDE or discrete update rule.
- A derivation of Einstein’s equation (or a rival).
- A definition of rods/clocks from the medium that is free of circularity.
- A proof that galactic curves follow without particulate dark matter.

Those are jobs for the four approaches below.

### 2.6 Slogan form of the seed

> **Mass is field locked; \(c\) is free-field locality, constant in our frame;
> energy is the continuum ledger; warp is what constant local \(c\) looks like
> around locks.**

### Approach A — Forward theoretical / relational

**Goal:** Write a continuum (or relational structure) and *derive* mass-form,
\(E = mc^2\) with locality-\(c\), and warp from constant local \(c\).

**Spirit:** axioms first; geometry and particles as theorems.

#### A1. Locality-first axioms

Postulate only:

1. A continuum with local states (free / bound / mixed).
2. A local causal bound \(c\) for free updates (locality).
3. A lock operation: free medium can enter bound configurations with a
   stability condition.
4. A conserved total medium budget (or a precise identity free + bound).

Derive:

- rest energy of a lock \(E_{\mathrm{bound}}\);
- inertial mass \(m = E_{\mathrm{bound}}/c^2\);
- effective null structure for free signals around locks when observers enforce
  local \(c = \mathrm{const}\).

**Relational flavor:** do not start with \(\mathbb{R}^{1,3}\) coordinates.
Start with an event set and a locality relation (“can influence within
bound \(c\)”). Metric emerges from that relation given locks.

#### A2. Medium hydrodynamics / condensate monism

Treat free field as a continuum fluid or condensate with density \(\rho\),
local sound/light speed \(c(\rho)\) or fixed \(c\) with variable optical index
from depletion. Bound mass = phase-locked droplet of the *same* fluid.

Relational content: two regions related by how much free medium lies between
them and how locks occlude paths — not by embedding distance first.

#### A3. Pure geometrodynamics with “mass = geometric lock”

No matter sector. Locks are topological or constraint-satisfying regions of
geometry itself (geon-like, but meeting monist budget: free geometric degrees
of freedom are reduced inside the lock). Revisit Wheeler-style programs with
the **depletion identity** as the missing piece (v8 geons failed partly
because they stayed dualist energy-in-geometry without free-budget lock).

#### A4. Process / relational monism (Mach-like)

Mass of a lock is defined only relative to the rest of the free medium
(inertia as relational resistance). \(c\) is the maximum signalling rate in the
web of relations. Curvature language is a summary of changed relation counts
around locks.

#### A — success criteria

- \(c\) defined from field/locality alone.
- \(m = E/c^2\) derived, not assumed as dualist matter.
- Warp around mass is a **theorem** from “local \(c\) constant + locks,” not an
  extra field equation bolted on.
- No fixed background metric as an independent actor.

#### A — failure modes to watch

- Secretly reintroducing a fixed chart and calling it “emergent.”
- Defining \(c\) from an a priori Minkowski metric (circular).
- Locks that do not reduce free budget (no depletion → no monist gravity).

### Approach B — Forward numerical

**Goal:** Implement a **medium** (not a field on a fixed stage that pretends to
be the medium) whose evolution can form locks; measure free-budget deficit and
ray / clock behavior under **local** \(c = \mathrm{const}\).

**Spirit:** postulate discrete/continuum rules; let structures form; refuse to
add gravity as a second pass.

#### B1. Causal cellular / graph medium

- State per node: free amplitude / locked flag / budget.
- Update only within locality radius (defines \(c\) on the graph).
- Lock rule: regions that satisfy a stability condition freeze budget into
  bound form; neighbors see less free budget.
- Measure: null geodesics as fastest free paths; time dilation as tick rates of
  local free oscillators; effective \(m\) as bound budget \(/c^2\).

No floating \(\phi\) on \(\mathbb{Z}^3\) with fixed Euclidean distance as truth —
distance must be **path cost in free medium** (or equivalent).

#### B2. Variable free-density continuum (optical monism)

Evolve a density \(\rho_{\mathrm{free}}(x,t)\) plus a bound density
\(\rho_{\mathrm{bound}}\) with exchange terms (formation / evaporation of mass).
Propagate waves with **local** speed fixed to \(c\) in the wave’s proper
normalization, but through a refractive structure set by \(\rho_{\mathrm{free}}\).

Test: does a bound clump produce deflection / delay that scales with
\(E_{\mathrm{bound}}/c^2\)? Does free density drop where bound density rises?

#### B3. Self-consistent effective metric from locks only

Evolve lock configurations; define \(g_{\mu\nu}\) *only* as the metric that
makes free-signal speed \(c\) locally; integrate rays. Compare to Newtonian /
Schwarzschild weak-field expectations. **Do not** feed \(T_{\mu\nu}\) into
Einstein by hand — if Einstein appears, it must be diagnostic of the medium,
not an input.

#### B4. Frame enforcement experiment

Numerically enforce: every local observer measures the same \(c\). After placing
or forming mass-locks, reconstruct the only global geometry consistent with
that enforcement. That geometry *is* the “warp around mass.”

#### B — success criteria

- Free budget visibly decreases where mass-form increases (same simulation).
- Ray bending / clock shifts appear **without** a second gravity solver.
- Measured \(m\) from dynamics (inertia of locks) matches \(E_{\mathrm{bound}}/c^2\).
- Removing dualist stage (fixed distance) does not collapse the notion of \(c\).

#### B — failure modes

- Lattice artifacts that look like gravity.
- Using fixed-grid Q-balls and *interpreting* \(\int T_{00}\) as warp (category
  error; forbidden as “proof” per `PROBLEM.md`).
- Soft-coding Einstein or Poisson into the update rule while claiming monism.

#### B — relation to existing SCP code

Kernel-v3 / Q-balls may supply **inspiration** for lock stability ideas, but
they are not this experiment. A v76 numerical medium is a **new object**. No
`scp_sim` change is implied by brainstorming B.

### Approach C — Reverse theoretical / relational

**Goal:** Start from what we already know works (local \(c\), \(E \leftrightarrow m\),
light deflection, inertia, approximate Newton/Einstein) and **invert** for the
properties the medium *must* have.

**Spirit:** reverse-engineer monism from phenomenology and operational
definitions; kill dualist degrees of freedom that are not needed.

#### C1. Operational reverse of \(c\)

Given: every local lab measures the same maximum signal speed.  
Ask: what class of media admit a notion of “local frame” in which that is true,
yet admit locks that change global signalling?

Output: constraints on free vs bound splitting and on how rods/clocks are made
of the same medium.

#### C2. Reverse of \(E = mc^2\)

Given: inertia and rest energy match via \(c^2\).  
Ask: if mass is bound field, what must “bound” mean so that inertial resistance
and unlock energy share one number?

Output: required equality between (i) energy to disassemble a lock into free
field and (ii) energy equivalent of accelerating the lock — both set by
locality \(c\).

#### C3. Reverse of warp

Given: weak-field light bending \(\propto M/r\), Shapiro delay, etc.  
Ask: if warp is “constant local \(c\) around locks,” what free-budget profile
\(\rho_{\mathrm{free}}(r)\) (or relational path-cost profile) is **forced**?

Output: a functional
\(\rho_{\mathrm{free}} \leftrightarrow M\)
that any forward medium theory must reproduce — a target, not a solution.

#### C4. Reverse of “no dark dust required?”

Given: galactic rotation / lensing residuals.  
Ask: *if* those were free-medium residuals from mass formation history, what
global free-field arrangement would be necessary? What is **ruled out**?

Output: necessary large-scale medium conditions; clear falsifiers (when
particulate DM is still required even under monist freedom).

#### C5. Category reverse — strip dualism

List every structure in standard GR+QFT that assumes stage vs actor. For each,
ask: can it be rewritten as free vs bound field with local \(c\)? If not, it is
either emergent bookkeeping or a sign monism fails.

#### C — success criteria

- A short list of **necessary conditions** any monist medium must satisfy
  (stronger than `PROBLEM.md` §7, phenomenology-loaded).
- Explicit map: observed \(M\), deflection angle, … → required free-budget
  contrast.
- Identification of which GR features are *identical* to constant-local-\(c\)
  bookkeeping vs which need extra structure.

#### C — failure modes

- Fitting GR by assuming GR (circular reverse).
- Smuggling stress-energy of “matter fields on spacetime” back in as the
  definition of mass.

### Approach D — Reverse numerical

**Goal:** Treat target phenomenology as **data**; invert or optimize a medium
model until local \(c\), mass locks, and warp cohere — then inspect whether the
recovered medium is monist and non-circular.

**Spirit:** computational reverse-engineering; adversarial tests against dualist
fakes.

#### D1. Ray-tomography of free medium

Input: synthetic or real lensing / delay maps around known mass.  
Invert for \(\rho_{\mathrm{free}}(x)\) or path-cost field under the assumption
“local \(c\) fixed.”  
Check consistency with bound mass from \(E_{\mathrm{bound}} = mc^2\).

#### D2. Lock library search

Enumerate lock rules (cellular, spectral, topological). Score each by:

- stable mass-form,
- free-budget drop,
- ray deflection scaling,
- local \(c\) invariance.

Keep only rules that win **without** an external Poisson/Einstein solve.

#### D3. Dualist adversarial baseline

Train or fit a dualist fake: fixed grid + Q-ball-like energy + separate gravity
solver. Demand monist candidates beat it on a score that **penalizes** second
sectors (Occam on ontology, not only fit to orbits).

#### D4. Frame-calibration inversion

From simulated “observer data” (local measurements of \(c\), clock rates, angles),
reconstruct the only medium state consistent with those reports. Compare to the
hidden generative medium. Success = recovery of free/bound split and locks.

#### D5. Galactic-scale reverse sims (late)

Only after weak-field monism works: ask what free-medium residual fields fit
rotation curves under mass-formation histories — and whether they remain
natural or become as epicyclic as dust DM.

#### D — success criteria

- Recovered medium shows **same** degrees of freedom for mass and warp.
- Inversion fails when forced to pure dualism with no free-budget link.
- \(m\) vs ray-inferred “gravitating mass” match via \(c^2\) without hand tuning.

#### D — failure modes

- Underdetermined inversions (many media fit one lens map).
- Numerics that “discover” Poisson because the loss function encodes it.
- Overclaiming cosmology before local monism works.

        A (forward theory)  ←—— constraints ——→  C (reverse theory)
                │                                      │
                │ rules                                │ targets
                ▼                                      ▼
        B (forward numerics) ←—— score / kill ——→  D (reverse numerics)

## 5. Worked micro-sketch (locality-\(c\), not a full theory)

This is an illustration of how the seed might *sound* in equations — **not**
claimed dynamics.

**Budget identity**

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{free}}(x) + \rho_{\mathrm{bound}}(x) = \rho_0
\quad\text{(or a known function of state)}.
\]

**Locality**

Free signals satisfy a local eikonal with speed \(c\) in the observer’s
orthonormal frame:

\[
g_{\mu\nu}(x)\, k^\mu k^\nu = 0,
\quad
c\ \text{fixed by free-field update law}.
\]

Here \(g_{\mu\nu}\) is **defined** as whatever makes that statement true given
\(\rho_{\mathrm{free}}\) and locks — not an independent Einstein tensor source
term.

**Mass**

\[
m = \frac{1}{c^2}\int \mathcal{E}[\rho_{\mathrm{bound}}]\,dV_{\mathrm{op}},
\]

with operational volume/time from free-field rods/clocks.

**Warp slogan**

Observers who keep local free speed \(= c\) will assign nontrivial \(g_{\mu\nu}\)
wherever \(\rho_{\mathrm{bound}} > 0\) forces \(\rho_{\mathrm{free}}\) (or free
paths) away from uniformity.

The open work is to replace this sketch with a closed, non-circular system that
passes `PROBLEM.md` §7.

- **Energy** — ledger of causal / reconfiguration capacity.  
- **Mass** — bound field / \(c^2\).  
- **\(c\)** — free-field locality, constant in our frame.  
- **Warp** — global face of constant local \(c\) around locks.

**Four approaches**

| | Theory | Numerics |
|--|--------|----------|
| **Forward** | A: axioms of medium → mass & warp | B: evolve medium → measure deficit & rays |
| **Reverse** | C: phenomena → necessary medium laws | D: invert / score media against targets & dualist fakes |

> Because \(c\) (field locality) is constant in our frame, locking field into
> mass-form *must* make free-field paths look warped around mass — if monism is
> true. The work is to make that statement into closed math and killable
> simulations, not to improve particles on a fixed fabric.
