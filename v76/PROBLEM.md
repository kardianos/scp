## 0. One-sentence problem

**Prove a single geometric continuum in which mass-form *is* fabric locked into
particle structure, free fabric is *less* where mass has formed, and that
deficit *is* the gravitational / lensing structure — with no second substance
and no secondary “energy tells spacetime how to curve” step as ontology.**

---

## 1. Dualism vs monism

### 1.1 The usual story (dualism)

1. There is **spacetime** (the stage).
2. There is **stuff** on it (particles / fields / energy).
3. Stuff has energy → energy **tells** the stage how to bend:

\[
G_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4}\, T_{\mu\nu}.
\]

\(E = mc^2\) sits in that story as: the stuff’s rest mass *is* energy. Energy is
still treated as something *of the particle* (or *of the fields on the stage*),
which then acts *on* geometry.

That language is dualistic even when the “stuff” is called a field: the metric
(or grid) remains an independent carrier of distance, and \(T_{\mu\nu}\) is a
source *for* geometry.

### 1.2 The claim to be tested (monism)

There is only the **field / fabric**.

A particle is not a customer of spacetime. It is a **mode of the fabric**:

- localized,
- phase-locked / charge-carrying / topologically or dynamically locked,
- energetically self-bound.

So it is not:

> energy of a particle → curves space.

It is:

> **field taken into particle form** = a constrained, self-sustaining
> deformation of the same continuum.

The “curve” (gradients, flux, stress, winding, effective metric, null
structure, …) **is the particle-form**, not a secondary reaction to it.

Outside the core, long-range structure is not “the particle’s influence on
empty space.” It is the **same continuum**, continuous with the core, forced
into a nontrivial configuration by mass-form bookkeeping.

Under monism:

| Bookkeeping | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| \(m = E/c^2\) | How much fabric energy is locked in that particle-form |
| \(\int T_{00}\,dV\) | Integral of the **same** continuum’s stress — a ledger, not a second substance |
| Free fabric near mass | **Less**, because that fabric *became* (or was locked into) mass-form |
| Lensing / attraction | Geometry of that rearranged medium — identity, not post-hoc coupling |

Mass is the energy of the arrangement. “Curvature” is the shape of the
arrangement. **Same object, two measurements.**

## 2. Core physical claims (tight form)

1. **One continuum** — the field/fabric. No second layer of “matter on space.”
2. **Mass is fabric in particle-form** — not energy *of* a particle that then
   acts *on* fabric.
3. **Formation of mass is redistribution of the medium**  
   Where mass is, free (non-mass) fabric is **less**, because that fabric
   *became* the mass-structure.
4. **Curvature / attraction is not a secondary sourcing law as ontology**  
   Not \(T_{\mu\nu} \to G_{\mu\nu}\) as “stuff tells geometry.”  
   The geometric effect **is** the depletion / locking of the medium into
   mass-form.
5. **Large-scale effects** (lensing, horizons, galactic rotation residuals)
   can be **medium geometry of mass formation**, not necessarily extra
   particulate dark matter.

That is stronger than a wording change. It is a **different kinematics and
conservation law**.

## 3. Why existing structures cannot prove this

### 3.1 Fixed-background field theory (including SCP kernel-v3)

A standard action

\[
S[\phi, A;\, g_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{fixed}}]
\]

or evolution on a fixed lattice already assumes:

| Built-in assumption | What it forces |
|---------------------|----------------|
| Ambient space exists independently | Fabric is a *stage* |
| Fields are values *on* points | Particles are excitations *on* space |
| Energy density \(T_{00}[\phi]\) | Bookkeeping of those excitations |
| Optional later: couple \(T\) to curvature | Curvature is *secondary* to field energy |

**Ontology, math, and geometry are the same failure.** Relabeling “the field
is the particle” while keeping a fixed metric/grid does not test monism. The
medium that could be depleted into mass was never dynamical as geometry.

### 3.2 Q-balls are the wrong species of object for this claim

A Q-ball (v66–v74) is a success **inside dualism’s engineering frame**:

- stable localized \(\Phi\) with conserved \(Q\),
- binding, fusion, Coulomb dressing on a fixed grid,
- mass as total field energy \(m \approx E = \int e\,dV\).

It does **not** test monism:

- the grid metric is fixed;
- the core is typically **more** field amplitude, not a demonstration that
  **free** fabric is less;
- lensing / null structure / galactic medium effects are absent by construction;
- gravity, if added later as \(T \to G\), reimports the secondary sourcing step
  the claim rejects.

**Judgment:** Q-balls, fixed-background Lagrangians, and multi-fabric bags on
shared flat space are **not fit for purpose** for proving this claim. They can
remain useful for particle engineering. They cannot *prove* “mass is fabric,
curvature is mass-formation.” Proving the second with tools built for the first
is a category error.

### 3.3 What v66–v75 actually proved (and what they did not)

| Achieved | Not achieved (this problem) |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Fields can look like stable particles | Particles *are* dynamical fabric geometry |
| Conserved charge, nuclei, forces on a grid | Free fabric depleted into mass-form |
| Multi-fabric path toward atoms (v75) | Lensing / horizons as medium identity |
| Structural analogs of nuclear bookkeeping | Dark-matter-like effects from mass formation |

Partial positive outcomes on the particle ladder are **orthogonal** to a
positive outcome for monist gravity-as-mass-formation. Different theorems.

## 4. The mathematical difference (eligibility, not a finished theory)

A candidate is even *eligible* only if mass-form and free fabric share one
budget and one dynamics.

### 4.1 Shared medium budget

Something of the form

\[
\rho_{\mathrm{free}} + \rho_{\mathrm{bound\ in\ mass\hbox{-}form}}
= \rho_{\mathrm{total}}
\]

(or a stricter geometric identity), **not**

\[
g_{\mu\nu}\ \mathrm{fixed},\qquad
m = \int T_{00}[\phi]\,dV_{\mathrm{flat}}
\]

as the definition of mass.

### 4.2 Particle-form as a state of the medium

Mass is a **constrained configuration** of the continuum (defect, condensate
lock, topological trap, self-consistent slow region, …) such that:

- free propagating degrees of freedom are reduced there,
- exterior geometry / wave speed / effective metric is altered **by that
  reduction**,
- the alteration is *identical* to “having mass,” not caused by it afterwards.

### 4.3 One evolution, not two sectors

Not: solve \(\phi\) on flat space → compute \(T\) → feed Einstein.  
Rather: **one evolution** in which forming a mass-structure *is* rearranging
the medium, and geodesics / null rays / pressure follow that rearranged medium.

Einstein’s equation may appear as an *effective* description of medium stress
vs geometry — only if both sides are aspects of one object, not a matter sector
plus a geometry sector.

### 4.4 Observables that only this class can pass

| Observable | Fixed-background Q-ball | Medium monism (required) |
|------------|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Stable lump, charge, fusion | Yes | Possible; different object |
| Local energy ledger ≈ mass | Yes | Yes — as medium lock-in |
| Free field **depleted** where mass formed | No (core is usually high \(\|\phi\|\); vacuum is empty of \(\phi\), not “less fabric”) | **Required** |
| Light deflection from medium deficit | Absent unless metric dynamical | **Required** |
| Galactic-scale mass-like effect without extra particles | No | Possible if mass-formation rearranges large-scale medium |
| Horizon as medium breakdown / total lock-up | No | Possible extreme of formation |

**Critical contrast:** in a Q-ball, the core is **more** field amplitude. In
this monism, mass *is* field, so **free** medium should be **less** where mass
has formed. That is almost the opposite visual of “big \(\Phi\) blob on empty
vacuum,” unless “blob” is redefined as *bound* medium and the *propagating*
substrate is another projection of the **same** continuum.

## 5. Large-scale consequences (program, not theorems)

Under this monism the causal chain is:

```text
mass formation
  → medium redistributed / locked into particle-form
    → free fabric less in/near that region
      → wave speed / null structure / pressure changes
        → lensing, orbital dynamics, possible horizons
```

Then, as **research hypotheses** (to be derived or killed by a real medium
theory, not by dualist particles on a grid):

- **Lensing** — rays through a medium whose free density/geometry was altered
  by mass-form.
- **Black hole** — extreme mass-formation: free fabric cannot support ordinary
  propagation (trap / horizon of the medium).
- **“Dark matter”** — mass-like gravitational effect from **how** mass formed
  and how the medium was left globally — not necessarily unseen particulate
  dust.

None of these is established. All of them are *the kind of prediction* a monist
medium theory must eventually face. None can be settled by Q-ball campaigns.

## 6. The correct question

**Not:**

> How do we get particles and gravity from fields on spacetime?

**But:**

> What single geometric continuum has states that we call “mass,” such that  
> (i) mass-form consumes free fabric,  
> (ii) the deficit *is* the gravitational / lensing structure, and  
> (iii) ordinary particle phenomenology is the *local* locked state of that
> continuum —  
> with no second substance and no secondary “\(T\) sources \(G\)” step as
> ontology?

**Proof bar:**

> Exhibit dynamics where **removing free fabric into mass-form** produces the
> observed geometric effects **as identity**, not as post-hoc coupling of a
> soliton’s energy to an external metric.

Until that object exists, every success with Q-balls, Lagrangians on fixed
space, or multi-fabric bags is **orthogonal progress**: better particle
engineering on a stage — not a proof that stage and actor are one.

## 7. Eligibility criteria (fit for purpose)

A candidate theory is eligible for *this* problem only if:

1. **No fixed background metric** as the independent carrier of distance  
   (or: metric / null structure is *defined from* the medium state).
2. **A conservation or identity** tying free medium to mass-form  
   (depletion is not optional rhetoric).
3. **Mass is not defined as** \(\int T_{00}\) of an independent field on flat
   space; that integral may appear only as an approximation after geometry is
   fixed by the medium.
4. **Null / timelike structure** responds to mass-formation without a second
   “gravity sector” as an ontological add-on.
5. **Particle stability** is stability of a **medium lock**, not only
   Vakhitov–Kolokolov stability of \(\phi\) on \(\mathbb{R}^3\).
6. **Discriminating predictions**: e.g. lensing or inertia tied to medium
   deficit, not only to \(\int T_{00}\); large-scale residuals from formation
   history of the medium, not only from extra dust.

Standard Q-ball / kernel-v3 math fails (1)–(5) by construction.

## 10. Bottom line

The concept is not “particles are fields, therefore \(E = mc^2\), therefore GR.”

It is:

> **Mass-form = fabric used up into structure; free fabric is less there; that
> less-ness *is* the geometric effect.**

Proving that requires a continuum where **depletion and geometry are the same
degree of freedom** — not better seeds for Q-balls on a grid.

v76 exists to hold that problem cleanly until a fit-for-purpose math exists.
