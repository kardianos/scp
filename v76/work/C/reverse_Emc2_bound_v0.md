### 1.1 Unlock energy

For an isolated lock \(L\) in its rest frame, define

\[
E_{\mathrm{unlock}}[L]
\;=\;
\text{minimum work (free-medium ledger) to return \(L\) to unbound free medium}
\]

with free radiation allowed to leave; asymptotic free state = no lock.

\[

\;=\;
m_{\mathrm{inertial}}\,\mathbf{a}
\;+\;
\mathcal{O}(v^2/c^2)
\;+\;
\text{(radiation reaction, medium drag residuals)}.
\]

\(m_{\mathrm{inertial}}\) is defined by that response, not by a primitive particle attribute.

### 1.3 Locality \(c\)

\[
c
\;=\;
\sup\{\text{free-update causal speeds in local free frames}\}.
\]

## 3. Necessary meaning of “bound”

### B-def (necessary)

A region of medium is **bound (mass-form)** to ledger \(E_\star\) when:

1. **Non-participation:** Some continuum capacity in that region does **not** participate in free causal updates at the full free locality (locked channels / frozen budget / trapped topology / phase-locked condensate — mechanism open).

2. **Unlock threshold:** Restoring that capacity to free participation requires supplying at least \(E_\star\) of free-medium reconfiguration work (definition of \(E_{\mathrm{unlock}}\)).

3. **Single-ledger inertia:** The same capacity is what the free medium must “drag” or rearrange under acceleration; the quadratic cost of slow motion is \(\frac12 (E_\star/c^2) v^2\).

4. **No residual dual mass:** There is no extra inertial label independent of \(E_\star\).

If (1)–(2) hold but (3) uses a different functional of the lock, monism fails the reverse \(E=mc^2\) test.

## 4. Why the factor \(c^2\) is forced

### 4.1 Only free velocity scale

Free medium offers one universal speed \(c\) for causal rearrangement. Energy and mass dimensions related by velocity\(^2\) therefore use \(c^2\):

\[
[m] = [E]/[v]^2
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
m = E_\star / c^2
\text{ if } c \text{ is the medium’s velocity scale}.
\]

### 4.2 Causal sketch (not a full proof)

Slow acceleration over time \(T\), velocity change \(v\ll c\): free medium must reorganize around the lock within causal domain size \(\sim c T\).  
If the locked ledger is \(E_\star\), the minimal free-medium kinetic-like cost that is quadratic in \(v\) and linear in \(E_\star\), built only from \(c\), is

\[
\delta E_{\mathrm{kin}}
\;\sim\;
E_\star \cdot \frac{v^2}{c^2}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
m_{\mathrm{inertial}} = \frac{E_\star}{c^2}.
\]

(Pre-factor \(\frac12\) is convention of kinetic definition; the structural claim is proportionality \(E_\star/c^2\).)

### 4.3 What would break it

- An independent inertial scale \(m_0\) not proportional to unlock energy.  
- Unlock energy stored in a sector that free medium does not couple to for acceleration (hidden dualist sector).  
- Using a different speed \(c_s \neq c\) for inertia while light uses \(c\) without a relation (two localities → two “masses”).

Reverse theory **requires** integral (or equivalent) conservation of ledger under isolated formation.  
It does **not** require pointwise \(\rho_f+\rho_b=\rho_0\) (that choice is constrained separately by C3 path-cost tails).
