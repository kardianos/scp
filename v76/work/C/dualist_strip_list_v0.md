## Rewrite test (apply to each candidate A/B medium)

For every primitive \(P\) in a proposal:

1. Is \(P\) a state of the one continuum, or a second object?  
2. If free/bound language is removed, does \(P\) still do independent work?  
3. Does removing the “gravity pass” kill the warp? If yes, \(P\) was dualist scaffolding.

---

## Keep as *emergent bookkeeping* (OK if derived)

- Effective \(g_{\mu\nu}\) for free rays under local \(c\).  
- Effective \(G_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from free-response kernel.  
- Effective Newtonian potential \(\Phi\) with \(\delta n \sim -2\Phi/c^2\).  
- Multipole expansions of path cost.  
- Particle-like quantum numbers of locks (charge, spin) **if** they are lock invariants of the medium.

Emergent is allowed. Independent actor is not.

---

## Hard fails (instant dualism)

1. Fixed metric + independent matter fields as complete ontology.  
2. Mass defined only as flat \(\int T_{00}\).  
3. Warp only after external Einstein/Poisson solve.  
4. Inertia not equal to unlock ledger \(/c^2\).  
5. Local free speed set by chart, not free medium.

---

## Bottom line

Strip stage/actor, two-sector actions, primitive mass, fixed-grid distance-as-truth, and bolt-on \(T\to G\).  
Keep free vs bound, local \(c\), single ledger \(E_\star\), and path cost as free-medium structure.
