## 0. Verdict in five lines

1. The mechanism the papers prove belongs to the right class. A field
   whose classical energy has no scale generates one from its
   fluctuations. v98 1B already found that the fiber's Dirichlet
   energy has no scale (`v98/phase1/DECIDE.md` §2). That is the
   precondition.
2. The mechanism needs a non-abelian fiber. The law's fiber is U(1):
   one complex amplitude per cell, with a linear hop that commutes
   with a global phase (`gap.out` H1–H4, errors near 1e-15).
3. On a U(1) fiber, the gap comes from the same phase slips that
   change `W`. A generated size and a protected wrap exclude each
   other. A generated `ξ` caps the wrap at about `π²ξ`; it does not
   pick `n` (`gap.out` W). This is the v93 ring record seen from the
   other side: the vacuum holds `W`, and a medium dephases it in
   about 10 t.u.
4. A non-abelian fiber needs three new premises and gives up integer
   winding. Its size is exponentially sensitive to the coupling:
   `ξ(2π)/ξ(2) ≈ 4×10⁵`. Any normalization convention is then `ρ`
   again, in a worse form.
5. The Haldane route, where an integer fixes the coupling, needs spin
   1/2 on each link end. v100 G2 just refused that as an import.

**Not promising on held theory. Not opened.**

## 1. The idea

v99 Phase 1 ended at C partly because a free Gaussian cannot set its
own scale; its `m` is always an input (`RELENT.md` §4).
`phase1/DECIDE.md` §4 refuses `S_rel` of a *Gaussian* wrap. It does
not refuse a scale generated by interaction. An interacting
two-dimensional field can produce a correlation length with no mass
parameter (dimensional transmutation). The candidate: the fiber
field, with interaction, generates `ξ`, and the lock's size is `ξ`.

The fiber is two-dimensional either way. In v98 1B's continuum it is
2-D. In v99's discrete version it is a `C_n` ring times time. Two
dimensions is where the results below apply.

## 3. The fork: which symmetry the law gives the fiber

`v89/PRINCIPLE.md` §3: a link actuates in two transverse planes with
a relative phase. In the kernel (`v97/kernel/freecell.c` pass F):

- `fa1`, `fa2` are the two real parts of one complex amplitude
  `z = fa1 + i·fa2`.
- The self-rotation is `z → e^{−i·ang} z`.
- The hop is `z_i' = cos τ·z_i − i sin τ·z_j` (a beamsplitter).
- Both are linear and commute with a global phase. `gap.out` checks
  this against the transcribed code to 1e-15.

Every live document that writes the fiber uses one phase θ: v98 1B,
v99 Phase 1 (`φ = A cos θ`, `χ = A sin θ`), and v101 exp2. v100's
SU(2) frame was the exception, and it closed with the hedgehog
ruling. The only place the kernel holds two complex amplitudes per
cell is the v94 door register (`fa`, `χ`), whose revival is refused
(`PRINCIPLES.md` §5).

**The fiber is abelian.**

**X. Sensitivity of a transmuted size (215, O(4)).**

A factor of π in normalization moves `ξ` by 4×10⁵. At β = 2π, a 1%
change in β moves `ξ` by 21%. v99 §5 found `n = 6` against `n ≈ 40`
from two unit choices. Transmutation turns the same ambiguity into
five orders of magnitude.

**W. Winding on a U(1) ring** (bond weight `e^{K cos δ}`, exact
convolution). The winding free energy `ΔF(n)` matches `2π²K_eff/n`
and falls monotonically. There is no interior extremum.

| K | ξ | `π²ξ` | ΔF(32) | ΔF(128) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 2.78 | 27 | 0.82 | 0.20 |
| 8 | 14.9 | 147 | 4.59 | 1.15 |

The wrap is distinguishable (ΔF > 1) only for `n ≲ π²ξ`. That is a
ceiling. Ceilings are the class Face C and MONO_FINAL already closed.

## 6. A note on the gate

In the world, this mechanism does not fix size in units of the UV
scale either. Λ_QCD in Planck units depends on α_s at a high scale.
What transmutation fixes is one line, plus the dimensionless ratios
among physical quantities. If the size gate stays "force `n*` in
grain units with no coefficient," this class cannot pass it. Plausibly
nothing resembling the world's mechanism can. Keeping or changing
that gate is the user's decision. This page does not change it.

## 8. What is now true, and what is not

**True:**

1. The law's fiber field is abelian and free (U(1), linear hop).
2. On an abelian fiber, a generated correlation length caps a wrap;
   it does not select one.
3. A non-abelian transmuted size is exponentially sensitive to its
   coupling.
4. A gapped local door cannot select a size beyond `ξ` (265,
   consistent with `RELENT.md` §4).

**Not true:**

- A size was derived.
- `ξ ≈ 6` (Haldane) is a cell count.
- The papers authorize a kernel or law change.
- v99 Phase 3 is authorized.
