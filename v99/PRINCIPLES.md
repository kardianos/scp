## 2. Relative entropy is the finite comparison

Casini: von Neumann entropy of a spatial region is ill-defined in
QFT (type III). Relative entropy against a reference state is
finite. Connected-`Em` census is the regional quantity (Phase 2
`broad`). The lock, if it is informational, is

\[
S^{\mathrm{rel}}(\omega_{W=0}\|\omega_{W=\pm 1})
\]

on a **proper** subsystem — a grain, an edge, or an arc of the
fiber. Globally two pures give 0 or ∞.

This licenses `S_rel` as a *quantity the campaign may compute*.
Phase 1 computed it. It does not license `S_rel` as a variational
well, an action, or a cell field.

  not produce a unique interior `n*` other than v98’s already
  unforced `ρ = 1` tie.

  on `n = 3`.

  `ΔE_w + ε`) never stops: the wrap hoards.

  **independent of `n`**. Distinguishability of amplitude, not of

- `ω_1 = 1 ⇒ n = 6` is a unit choice. `ω_1 = T_{\rm Unruh}` is
  `n ≈ 40`. Neither is forced.

So: **the split is held; the selector is refused.** Unique size is
still the first mass question. This method did not answer it.
