## 1. What was asked

v98’s remaining premise: force `ρ`, or state a selector 1A/1B
already refused. v99 named one selector: relative entropy of a
`W = ±1` wrap against vacuum on a proper door, with no imported
area law.

Four possible endings, written in advance:

| ending | condition | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A | `S_rel` plus one energy forces a unique `n*` (or a short even ladder) with no leftover coefficient | that `n*` is a size law; a Phase 3 brief may be written, not opened |
| B | `S_rel` *is* `E_w` (same slide) | selector refused as a rename; v98 1B stands |
| C | every closed identity hoards, sits on `n = 3`, or reduces to unforced `ρ` / a unit choice | **stop this selector**; representation split may still be held |
| D | the unfold ledger is contradicted | veto; this path dies |

Exactly one ending may be claimed.

## 2. What Phase 1 actually returned

**Not A.** No identity without an import produced a unique interior
`n*`. The only even-tie at `{4,6}` is v98’s `E_w + n` at `ρ = 1`.
The only `n = 6` is `ω_1 = 1`, a unit choice (`RELENT.md` §5).

**Not B.** 1-site and half-cycle `S_rel` at `m = 0.1`, `A = 1`,
*grow* with `n` while `E_w` *falls*. Casini-kernel `S/K ~ 0.3`,
not 1. The selector is a different function. It still does not
pick a line.

**Not D.** `v98/UNFOLD.md` is unchanged. No veto.

**C.** Every conservation identity that does not import 2π, `A/4`,
or `κ = 1` either (i) slides to the cutoff (`E_w + nε`;
`ΔE_w + ε < 0` on `n = 3..23`), (ii) sits on the smallest cycle
(`E_w/ε + n`, `S + n`, `S` alone), or (iii) is v98’s unforced
`ρ = 1`. A grain door at `m = 1` is independent of `n`.

**The decision is C.** The selector is refused. The representation
split is held as ontology (`PRINCIPLES.md` §1). No new kernel.
Phase 3 is not authorized.

That is a result. It is not a proton.

conservation identity whose only free number is `2π` from

is varied over a decade. Phase 1’s menu is in `RELENT.md` §3–§4.

computed. The TSVs say otherwise.

say. None is on the ledger today.
