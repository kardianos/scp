## Thesis under test

Two separate claims. Neither is a radial profile.

1. **Succession.** A mass is a tail call that lands. The last act of
   a completed cycle is the first act of the next, on a fresh word.
   A partial cycle converts nothing. A completed cycle books two
   action atoms and is deleted. The test is which short successions
   reappear after one such move.
2. **Octave.** Spin \(1/2\) is an octave holonomy. A \(1{:}1\) cycle
   returns at \(2\pi\). A \(2{:}1\) cycle returns at \(4\pi\) on the
   double-cover phase. An integer slip on the interval chain costs
   the core energy already measured for the gate. The chain is not
   a unison ring with a fractional twist.
