| clause | floor (from H0, so known stores cannot sneak through) |

| I/O spatially separated | ⟨\|sep\|⟩ ≥ 0.8 and opposite-half polar ≥ 0.25 |
| dM/dt ≈ 0 | \|ΔEm\|/⟨Em⟩ ≤ 0.20 over the window |
| dipole of J steady | ⟨\|Jdip\|⟩ ≥ 0.25 ⟨\|Circ\|⟩ and dir-stable ≥ 0.80 |
