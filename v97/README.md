# PART I — BACKGROUND

## I.1 The one law and four constraints

Energy is never destroyed. It only changes mode. Space is one of the
modes (`v89/PRINCIPLE.md`). Therefore: no background (nothing persists
and is merely re-valued); no imported field or species; everything
emergent from the fabric. Matter is converted space. Motion is regular
conversion, not displacement. There are no equations of motion in the
usual sense, because nothing has a trajectory.

## I.2 The substrate

A dynamical cell roster (cells born, die, jam, repack — no fixed
lattice) with three *arrays* per cell, which this version refuses to
treat as three substances (`PRINCIPLES.md` P1):

- field `fa1, fa2` (complex amplitude; `Ee = |ψ_field|²`) — unitary
  pairwise hops (pass F);
- dense `Em` + clock `th2` (now also a complex amplitude `ψ_m` when
  `amp_nat > 0`) — additive want at default, unitary hops behind
  `amp_nat`;
- space `Es` (radii `r ∝ ∛Es`) — additive pressure flow (pass S:
  pressure pushes, nothing reaches out).

Plus a **conversion door** (pass 6): field condenses at matter on a
clock beat above a work-function threshold; matter evaporates above
cap; the click is quantized into action atoms `ε = A₀ ω / 2π` with a
two-atom credit. Radiance taxes load `x = Em/cap` as `k_rad cap x^{p}`.
Conserved: `Em + Ee + Es` (+ `|χ|²` when the v94 register is live).
Battery drift bars witness this at the FP floor.

v94 added a per-cell register `χ` (mode-transit, default off). v95
applied the FLOW bed law to the dense link current (`j_metric`,
default off). Both are carried. Neither is the law.

## I.4 Why lump metrics failed

Transport, binding, and phase-retention are pairwise antagonists in
one hop (RING_DNLS synthesis). A tax truncates a hierarchy; it does
not mint a line (qgraph §7). A random bath fills the box to its own
load (Theorem 3.4). A sealed book at a point is π-flat (g4). A
cell-local door has no first moment (P2 ~100× recoil). qgraph's
shells and `Q = Z − N` are true of a *given* graph and silent about
which graph forms.

Those are theorems, not unlucky knobs. Scoring `Em_max` and PR
selects sealed bags and transients — successes at the wrong task.

## I.5 What correspondence is allowed to mean

No-background forbids an SI ruler. The only lawful comparison is a
**pure number** the theory is *forced* to produce, against a pure
number of the world (`PRINCIPLES.md` P4). A list of fabric ratios
parked next to 1836 is matching, not truth.

Truth is an identity: this process **is** what the lab calls mass, or
it is not. The six principles are the identities the campaign may
now try to close.

# PART II — THE SIX PRINCIPLES

Full text: [`PRINCIPLES.md`](PRINCIPLES.md). One line each, here, so
the charter stands alone.

1. **One energy, promotions not fluids.** `Ee` / `Em` / `Es` are
   occupations. The only matter operation is a promotion. Light is
   unlocked travel; mass is locked load; charge is incomplete
   capacity; exclusion is cap refusal.
2. **Pitches are harmonics of the chart, not law.** A gap is
   necessary (measured). `w1`, `w2` as table constants are not.
   `ω = √λ` of the current contact graph.
3. **Conversion proceeds where a cycle closes.** The regular method.
   Links promote or stall; contacts that never close, die. That is
   graph selection. The door stays grainy and moves onto the slot.
4. **The law table is debt.** One conventional scale, integers, the
   closure rule. Everything else awaits derive-then-retire. No SI.
5. **π-flatness of sealed matter is a prediction.** Rest-mass
   monopoles should be ~0. Galactic “dark mass” may be uncondensed
   texture. Local inertia is the open book of a live cycle. v96 W-A
   is this space-leg, not a new leak to mint Newton.
6. **Interaction starts as joint-graph energy, not as Coulomb.**
   Complementary deficits complete on contact. Long-range Maxwell
   is a later field-book question. Do not import a gauge field.
