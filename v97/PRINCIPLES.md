# v97 — the six principles

**Status:** adopted as the governing theory of v97 (user, 2026-08-12).
Not a law-table change. Not a kernel change. These are identity claims
the campaign is now allowed to make, and the constraints on how it may
try to make them true.

The four standing constraints are unchanged (`v89/PRINCIPLE.md`):
energy is never destroyed; no background; no imported field or species;
emergent from the fabric alone. What follows is how those constraints
are read after v89–v96, so correspondence to physics can be a true or
false statement rather than a unit choice.

Companions: `README.md` (charter), `METRICS.md` (the adopted hunt).

## P1 — One energy, promotions not fluids

`Ee`, `Em`, `Es` are occupations of one energy, not three substances.
The only matter operation is a **promotion** that pays the gap to the
other book. Emission and absorption are that operation read both ways
(the qgraph L3 algebra, lifted from a side library into the ontology).

Identities this licenses, if the campaign closes them:

| world | identity |
|---|---|
| photon | unlocked traveling occupation (transverse hop) |
| mass (the 99%) | locked / load-detuned occupation — converted space |
| inertia | dipole of a live promotion, not the store |
| charge | incomplete capacity on a cyclic chart (`Q = Z − N`) |
| spin | circulation of an unclosed doublet |
| exclusion | occupancy ceiling (cap refusal), not a Fermi field |

**Guard (v93 §IV.2 / §IV.7, still in force):** one norm, promotions only,
no per-component ledger, nothing that outlives the cell. This is fewer
identities than the three-fluid kernel, not more. It is not the quark
road.

The door stays grainy (atoms `ε = A₀ ω / 2π`, two-atom credit). It
becomes the promotion, not a pipe between tanks. Phase does not change
hands.

**Present kernel:** still three arrays plus the v94 register `χ`. Those
are implementation debt (P4), not the ontology.

## P2 — Pitches are harmonics of the chart, not law

A *gap* is the mode boundary. The battery already measured this:
collapse `w2` onto `w1` and conversion, atoms, and the fifth vanish
together (`v89/battery/README.md`). The *numbers* `w1 = 1.65` and
`w2 = 2.9` are not that fact.

The local pitch of each occupation is an eigenvalue of the *current*
contact graph, `ω = √λ`. Field and dense lock split because they occupy
different harmonics of the same chart, or because load shifts the chart
— not because the table contains two frequencies.

`w2 / w1` becomes a prediction about small surviving graphs, or the
idea is false. `cap` should suffer the same fate: an occupancy ceiling
in action atoms, not `2.5`. `p_rad = d_s + 1` is already written as a
truth claim (qgraph Theorem 4.1). P1 of that package failed it on
matter (`d_s ~ 1.1–1.6`) and passed it on foam (`~ 2.9`). That split is
data: a clump and a cavity are different graphs. Do not soften `p_rad`
to make them agree.

## P3 — Conversion proceeds where a cycle closes

This is Principle §7's own guess, adopted as the regular method.

- A **link** (not a cell) is allowed to promote when the two ends can
  close a joint cycle.
- It stalls when they cannot.
- Contacts that never close, die. That *is* graph selection.

Then “geometry is not quantized” is no longer a wall. Geometry is the
surviving cycle set. Magic numbers are partial capacities of *those*
graphs. Species are the small motifs that keep a cycle open against the
bath. Monodispersity is `Δ / T` on a selected graph.

The comb gate, `kappa_lock`, `p_gate`, `gamma_res`, `f_conv`, `f_evap`
become candidates to derive and retire, the way the choir already was.
Do not delete them on opening day. Write the closure rule so they are
either forced or unnecessary.

An atom with two ends has a first moment. Motion **is** that promotion
walking. Uptake and outtake **are** opposite orientations. Open books
**are** a polarized cycle. The P2 recoil deficit, π-flat point sources,
and door-scrambled spin are the same defect if the promotion sits on a
vertex: the conversion has no lever arm.

**The door is not unitarized.** Grain is the ħ identity. The change is
*where* it fires (oriented slot), not *whether* it is discrete.

## P4 — The law table is debt

A theory that can be true of this world has:

- **one conventional scale** (the action grain, or `c = 1` from the
  conversion rate — both identities already exist in prose)
- **integers** (two-atom credit, occupancy 0 or 1, dimension of space
  as a measurement)
- **the closure rule** (P3)

Everything else in `laws_V3a.cfg` is a remaining rate awaiting
retirement. Correspondence is then a short list of *forced* pure
numbers (pitch ratios of surviving motifs, capacity ladders,
`λ̂ /` width, load ratios, circulation quanta). Those match the
world's dimensionless numbers or they do not.

Until the table is that short, a match is suspicious and a miss is
meaningless. Do not map `Em*` to a proton. Do not import SI.

Carried knobs (`amp_nat`, `amp_door`, `door_g`, `j_metric`, `rad_dual`,
`χ`, …) stay default-0. They are instruments and candidates, not the
law. Battery 87 GREEN at defaults is the inertness proof, not a
physics proof of any of them.

## P5 — π-flatness of sealed matter is a prediction

A sealed book that opens and closes at the same cell sources no steady
space monopole. g4 / ASTRO measured this. It may be **true of the
world**, not a missing-1/r bug.

Consequence, stated as a claim: most of what astronomy calls
mass-at-range is field/space texture; condensed matter is the metabolic
minority. Local falling and inertia are owed to the *open* book of a
live cycle (and of moving, radiating bodies), not to a sealed rest-mass
monopole.

Pass S stays “pressure pushes, nothing reaches out.” A steady internal
space cycle at constant mass is not a new space fluid. Gradient flow of
`Es` is zero in static equilibrium; a constant-mass cycle has to be
the conversion current, not `−∇p`. If the door is on the slot (P3),
that current is already there.

v96's W-A (“add `Es → Em` consumption to mint 1/r”) is reinterpreted,
not rubber-stamped: space consumption is the space-leg of a polarized
promotion, scored by Es throughput at constant mass (`METRICS.md`),
not a new leak bolted onto a lump.

## P6 — Interaction starts as joint-graph energy, not as Coulomb

Do not add a force law for `Q`. The first interaction the one law
already permits: two incomplete occupations, brought into contact, can
share a chart whose joint harmonics are lower than the disconnected
pair. Opposite deficits complete; like deficits do not. That is
chemistry as graph surgery. It is contact-range. It is in-spirit.

Long-range Coulomb is a later question: whether an unclosed shell
leaves a *field-book* texture another such shell can feel. If yes,
electromagnetism is the field book of incomplete locks. If no, the
theory has quantum numbers and chemistry and does not have Maxwell —
an honest smaller theory.

Trying to get 1/r² from `Q` before the promotion lives on a link will
invent a gauge field. That is the imported species already banned.

## What these principles refuse

- A fourth fabric, a U(1), opposite-charge particles, SI mass mapping.
- Unitarizing the door (lose grain, lose ħ).
- Complexifying `Es` as a state relabel (the missing moment is the
  conversion current).
- Adopting `amp_nat`, `door_g`, `j_metric`, or `rad_dual` as if a
  better hop were a better ontology.
- Painting qgraph shells onto hoards as kernel law. Adopt the
  *promotion law*, not the wallpaper.
- Scoring persistent Em lumps as particles. The hunt is `METRICS.md`.
