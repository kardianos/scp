## What the instruments are

| # | meter | how |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Motif census | union-find on live-Em cells (`Em > 0.05`) via LINK contacts; tracked by wrapped centroid |
| 2 | Per-motif I / O | frame-to-frame, cells matched by position (not index): condensation-shaped `(ΔEm>0, ΔEe<0)` vs radiance/evap-shaped opposite; split fore/aft along J |
| 3 | Dipole of J | `J_ij = 2 Im(ψ_i* ψ_j)`, `ψ = √Em e^{iθ}`; Jnet, Jdip of cell monopoles, Circ on internal links; also global and tagged sums |
| 4 | Es throughput | pass-S pressure law on the motif surface and on the Em>0.5 **core** surface (the leak meter) |
| 5 | I/O separation | `(c_I − c_O) · ĵ`; 0 = point book |

CLI: `./dipole FILE.fcs` (TSV) · `./dipole -h0 sealed|leak|ring|bathring|current FILE.fcs`

Limits (named, not hidden): I/O is a mode-crossing *heuristic* (hops contaminate); snapshot J is not `fd`; Es flux is unclamped rate, not the kernel's outflow-limited move.
