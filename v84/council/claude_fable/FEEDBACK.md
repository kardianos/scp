## 1. Executive stance: PARTIAL ACCEPT

Accept as research basis: **A1** (one continuum), **A5** (process closure), **A6**
(charts as re-partitions), and the *program* of A2 (energy as vacuum-referenced
contrast). Accept the design consequence that v83-style multi-rev lock orbits were
never a mass test — that diagnosis is correct and the stop is right.

**Reject A3 as a definition; accept it only as a hypothesis.** "M = entanglement
content / locality quantum" is currently a ratio of two unmeasured, un-unitted
quantities, at least one of which (N-ENT) has no agreed meaning for a classical
lattice field, and the other of which (N-LQ) has two mutually inconsistent
candidate definitions that differ by whether they are object-properties or
regulator-properties. Until D1–D4 close, A3 is a slogan with a division sign.

**Strongest single objection:** §5.4 demotes the inertia test to "optional
consistency." That inverts the epistemology. Inertial response under known force
is what *mass means* operationally — in this project and in physics. If N-M
disagrees with measured inertia, N-M is not mass; it is a new quantity that
deserves a new name. The inertia measurement is the referee of the creed, not an
optional cross-check. I return to this in §6 (kill gates).

A second framing caution up front: **the creed changes the accounting, not the
dynamics.** Stage 3's actual blocker (v75–v83) is dynamical — no stable light
opposite-charge carrier exists in the model. Redefining mass cannot manufacture
one. The creed earns its keep only if the new accounting changes *which
experiments are worth running*, which it partially does (§2.1).

## 2. Part A — forward from the creed

Assume A1–A6. What follows?

### 2.1 What genuinely becomes possible

**(a) Mass becomes falsifiably distinct from energy.** Under dualist E=mc²,
M ∝ E with a universal constant, always. The creed predicts M tracks
*entanglement structure*, which need not be proportional to δE across objects.
This is the creed's one discriminating, decidable claim, and it dictates the
first experiment: find (or fail to find) two configurations with **equal δE and
different N-M**, or a scan (Q, ω, fusion state) where N-M/δE is *not* constant.
If N-M/δE is universal across everything measurable, the creed is empirically
decoration on E=mc² and should be retired (kill gate G2).

Concrete candidate triple, all in the existing read-only nuclear stack:
- single ball at Q,
- two parked balls at Q/2 each, far separated (same total charge, nearly same
  total δE, radically different correlation structure),
- fused droplet of the same total Q (He vs 2×N from v74).

Write the predicted N-M *ordering* under creed vs under E=mc² **before**
measuring. If the creed cannot predict an ordering here, A3 is not yet a theory.

**(b) The positronium analog becomes cheaper than v75 assumed.** The creed drops
the requirement that the light partner be a lock or a new fabric species (D4
prefers "fabric conjugate charge structure"). The conjugate ball Φ→Φ* (ω→−ω) is
an exact solution with identical δE by charge-conjugation symmetry — an
*equal-mass* opposite-charge partner exists for free. Positronium is an
equal-mass bound state, so **Stage 3's first success criterion (creed §3.4 item
2) is reachable with existing tools**: two conjugate balls, medium Coulomb
attraction (already validated by radial force scans), closed Gauss and process
ledgers. No kernel change, no locks, no multiplets. This should be the first
*dynamical* experiment of v84+ after the mass metrology exists.

**(c) The v83 failure ledger becomes coherent.** E1b/E2 failed *as orbit
engineering*; under the creed they were also category errors — testing lock
kinematics, never mass. The creed's reject list (§4) is the first version of
this project's Stage-3 story where the failures have a common diagnosis rather
than a list of bad luck. Keep that.

### 2.2 What the creed leaves underspecified (blocking)

**(i) N-ENT has no referent yet.** The simulations are deterministic classical
PDEs. A single classical field configuration has zero entropy, no density
matrix, and no entanglement in the technical sense. Every candidate in D3
secretly requires an *ensemble* to be chosen first, and the choice is the
physics:

- **Gaussian-fluctuation route** (my recommendation): linearize about the parked
  soliton, treat the fluctuation two-point functions as the covariance matrix of
  a Gaussian state, compute region entanglement entropy / mutual information
  from symplectic eigenvalues. Well-defined, standard, and connects to real QFT
  entanglement (§3.1b). Cost: it's the entanglement of the *fluctuations*, not
  of the background — the creed must own that reading.
- **Cycle-ensemble route:** treat one clock period's sweep of configurations as
  the ensemble (the process reading, consonant with v73 P1). Classical MI is
  then defined but its relation to quantum entanglement is by analogy only.
- **QFI-on-ρ_Q route** (the CONCEPT/FUTURE sketch): beware a degeneracy trap —
  for a coherent-state-like U(1) object, QFI with respect to the charge
  generator scales ∝ Q. If N-LQ = 2πQ (D2 process option), then
  N-M = QFI/2πQ ≈ **universal constant**: every object has the same mass and
  the definition is dead on arrival. This scaling audit must be done on paper
  before any pipeline is built (action #1, §5).

**(ii) N-LQ's two candidates are not two options; they are two ontologies.**
h_eff = 2πQ per cycle is an *object* property (measured, v73 P1 — the best
number in this program). The lattice causal cell c·Δt is a *regulator* property
that vanishes in the continuum limit and would make M cutoff-divergent (§3.2c).
Only the process option is admissible. But note what it implies: M = N-ENT/2πQ
is entanglement **per unit charge** — structurally a cousin of E/Q, which the
project already measures as a branch diagnostic. The creed should check it is
not about to rediscover E/Q with extra steps.

**(iii) The mass hierarchy is untouched.** The conjugate ball gives equal-mass
partners (good for positronium, §2.1b), but a carbon *atom* wants light
electrons around a heavy nucleus. The branch floor Q_min ≈ 80–88 against the
carbon droplet Q ≈ 921 gives at best ~1:10, nothing electron-like. The creed is
silent on where a light branch comes from. Acceptable for now (structural
analog, not quantitative), but Stage 4 planning must not assume the creed
solved this.

### 2.3 What kills the creed (Part-A view)

1. No candidate N-ENT survives the scaling/degeneracy audit → A3 has no
   operational content.
2. N-M/δE universal across the discriminating triple → creed is redundant.
3. N-M contradicts inertial response → creed-M is not mass.

Formalized as gates in §6.

### 2.4 How Stage 3–4 should proceed under the creed

Order of operations, design-level:

1. Mass metrology first (N-δE, N-LQ, N-ENT, N-M on the *heavy* stack, where
   objects are known-stable). Do not touch new carriers until the ruler exists.
2. Conjugate-pair positronium analog: bound, ledger-closed, both partners with
   measured N-M. This is Stage 3's honest first milestone and needs no new
   physics.
3. Only if (2) binds and N-M is non-degenerate: ask whether a light branch
   exists (a new-sector question — likely needs a design discussion and
   possibly kernel authorization; the creed does not pre-authorize it,
   correctly).
4. Stage 4 shell bookkeeping stays as CREED §2.3 states, but with equal-mass
   "electrons" acknowledged as a structural deviation until a light branch
   exists.

## 3. Part B — backward from QFT

Start from: particles as field quanta; m² as the Casimir of the Poincaré group
(pole of the two-point function); E² = p²c² + m²c⁴; renormalization;
entanglement in QFT; microcausality; composite bound states.

### 3.1 What in the creed is a plausible structural analog

**(a) "Mass is arrangement, not stuff" is QFT-orthodox for composites.** ~99% of
proton mass is field energy (gluon field + quark kinetic energy, trace
anomaly), not constituent rest mass. A2's vacuum-subtracted δ is structurally
the same move as normal ordering / vacuum subtraction in QFT. The heavy-stack
program (mass defect, E/Q, fusion binding) maps cleanly onto how hadron masses
actually work. This part of the creed needs no defense.

**(b) The energy–entanglement link has a real QFT anchor — but it points the
"wrong" way for A3.** The first law of entanglement entropy (Casini's
Bekenstein-bound proof; Jacobson's 2015 "entanglement equilibrium" derivation
of the Einstein equations) states, for small perturbations of ball-shaped
vacuum regions:

    δS_EE = δ⟨H_mod⟩ ≈ 2π R δE / (ħ c)   (Rindler/CFT normalization)

Read as the creed wants: *entanglement contrast = energy contrast per modular
quantum*, with ħ appearing exactly where the creed puts its "locality quantum."
This is the most respectable ancestor A3 could claim, and I recommend citing it
as the intended analog. **But note two consequences:**

1. The first law makes δS *proportional to δE* — so the closest true-QFT
   statement collapses M back onto E, which is exactly kill gate G2. The creed
   is betting that soliton interiors are in the *nonlinear* regime where the
   first law breaks and entanglement carries independent information. That bet
   should be stated explicitly in the creed; it is currently implicit.
2. The first law carries a factor of region size R: δS ∝ R·δE. So a naive
   N-M = ENT/LQ will scale like **E × size**, not E. Prediction: two objects of
   equal δE and different radius will give different naive N-M *for a
   first-law-trivial reason*. The discriminating experiment of §2.1(a) must
   control for this, or the creed will claim a false victory over E=mc² that
   is actually just the radius factor.

**(c) h_eff = 2πQ is semiclassically respectable.** In QFT, Q-ball charge is
quantized in units of ħ; the classical ω(Q) branch is the classical limit of a
quantum tower (Bohr–Sommerfeld on the internal rotation). The v73 measurement
∮2E_kin dt = 2πQ per cycle is the adiabatic invariant of that rotation. Calling
it a *candidate* process quantum (creed §2.4) is exactly right; calling it "the
ħ of QFT" would not be, and the creed correctly refrains.

### 3.2 What conflicts with QFT-tested structure

**(a) For classical solitons, E = mc² is a theorem, not a habit.** If the
action is Lorentz-invariant (the continuum Cosserat action is; the lattice
breaks it at O(Δx²)), then boosting a static solution gives E = γMc², p = γMv
with M ≡ E_rest/c². Derrick-type virial identities and the measured de Broglie
tilt (v70/v73 group-velocity results) are *consequences* of this. The project's
own past measurements (1–5% group-velocity lag, at lattice-artifact level)
confirm the model sits in this regime. Therefore, to lattice accuracy,
**inertia will equal δE/c² no matter what N-ENT says.** The creed cannot escape
this by demoting the inertia test; it can only reinterpret it: entanglement
structure may be *why* the energy is bound and localized (mechanism, order
parameter of mass-form), not an independent *amount* of mass. My
recommendation: restate A3 as

> "Entanglement per locality quantum is the **order parameter** that
> distinguishes mass-form from free fabric; its inventory is conjectured to
> determine the bound energy that inertia measures."

That version is testable, compatible with the soliton theorem, and preserves
everything the creed actually needs for Stage 3 design.

**(b) Mass is frame-invariant; entanglement entropy is not.** m² = P^μP_μ is a
Poincaré Casimir. Entanglement entropy of a region is observer-dependent (a
Rindler observer sees the Minkowski vacuum as thermal). Any N-ENT built from
equal-time regional data is a rest-frame quantity. The creed must therefore
stipulate: **N-M is defined in the object's rest frame (parked, envelope
static)**, and boost behavior is derived, not assumed. Silent frame-dependence
is how a "mass" definition quietly stops being a scalar.

**(c) Entanglement entropy in QFT is UV-divergent (area law).** S_EE ~
Area/ε² + …; the divergence is regulator-dependent and dominated by the
entangling surface. Translated to the lattice: a raw N-ENT will be dominated by
the surface area of the partition in lattice units — i.e., it measures the
*geometry of the cut*, not the object. Two mandatory consequences:

1. N-ENT must itself be a **δ-quantity**: (entanglement with object) minus
   (same partition, vacuum box, same N, L, Δx). A2's contrast principle must be
   applied to A3's numerator. The creed does not currently say this; it must.
2. The lattice-causal-cell option for N-LQ (D2) would make M explicitly
   cutoff-dependent — this is the QFT-backward reason to strike it, on top of
   the Part-A reason in §2.2(ii).

**(d) "Locality quantum" has no QFT counterpart.** In QFT, locality is a
*property* (microcausality: commutators vanish at spacelike separation), not a
countable unit. There is no quantum of locality to divide by. The nearest
respectable structures are (i) the modular-Hamiltonian ħ of §3.1(b), and
(ii) the semiclassical action quantum of §3.1(c). The creed should stop
implying the denominator is a piece of causal structure and commit to it being
a **process action quantum** — which D2's first option already is.

**(e) Renormalization: "bare vs dressed" is a warning label for D1.** In QFT
the vacuum subtraction and the mass definition are entangled with the regulator
(scheme dependence). The lattice analog: N-δE and N-ENT will both depend on
(N, L, Δx, snapshot cadence). A serious comparison requires a
**continuum-trend check** — measure N-M at two lattice spacings and show the
*ratio structure* (orderings, defect fractions) is stable even if absolute
values drift. Absolute N-M values will never be scheme-independent; ratios can
be.

### 3.3 Operational definitions needed for a serious QFT comparison

Minimum set, no quantitative carbon matching required:

1. **Ensemble declaration** for N-ENT (Gaussian-fluctuation covariance is the
   one with a genuine QFT counterpart — von Neumann entropy of Gaussian states
   from symplectic spectra of the two-point functions).
2. **Frame declaration:** rest frame of the parked object; boost behavior a
   measured, not assumed, property.
3. **Double subtraction:** vacuum-referenced energy (D1) *and*
   vacuum-referenced entanglement (same partition geometry).
4. **Units audit:** N-ENT in nats, N-LQ in action units → N-M has units of
   nats per action. State this and then state what "mass units" means, or
   accept that N-M is dimensionless-relational and only *ratios* of N-M are
   claims.
5. **First-law control:** the R·δE scaling of §3.1(b) computed and subtracted
   or bounded, so a nontrivial N-M/δE variation cannot be a size artifact.

## 4. Definitional risks (D1–D4)

**D1 (vacuum reference for δ) — LOW RISK, but currently vacuous.** Classically,
the empty-box vacuum has exactly zero energy, so N-δE ≡ ∫T₀₀ minus escaped
radiation — *identical to the bulk estimator the creed demotes*. A2 gains
content over the old definition only via (i) the process-ledger reading
(uptake/layment imbalance, v73 — real and measured) and (ii) the entanglement
subtraction of §3.2(c). Amend D1 to: the vacuum reference is mandatory for
**N-ENT**, where it is load-bearing, not only for energy, where it is trivial.
One real subtlety: with gauge on, a charged object's reference cannot be the
empty box (the Coulomb tail is part of the object's δ); state that the tail
inside the box is included and the absorbing-BC loss is ledgered.

**D2 (locality quantum) — HIGH RISK, resolvable now.** Strike the lattice
causal cell (regulator, not physics; §2.2(ii), §3.2(c)). Freeze
**N-LQ = h_eff = 2πQ per clock cycle**, the one measured candidate (v73 P1,
0.013–0.03%). Accept the consequence and write it down: N-M is then
entanglement per unit charge-action, an intensive cousin of E/Q, and total
inertial content = N-ENT itself with 2πQ as the unit. If that bookkeeping feels
circular when written out, that is D2 telling you A3 needs the §3.2(a)
order-parameter restatement.

**D3 (entanglement measure) — HIGHEST RISK. This is where the creed lives or
dies.** Three sub-risks: (i) *no ensemble* — a deterministic configuration has
no entropy; every candidate needs the ensemble declared first (§2.2(i));
(ii) *degeneracy* — QFI ∝ Q with N-LQ = 2πQ gives constant N-M (§2.2(i));
coherent-state-like objects are exactly the degenerate case; (iii) *area-law
contamination* — raw regional measures scale with cut geometry (§3.2(c)).
Requirement before freeze: a one-page **paper scaling audit** of each candidate
(expected dependence on Q, R, ω along the measured branch, thick- and thin-wall
limits). Any candidate that is provably constant, provably ∝ δE·R (first-law
trivial), or cut-dominated is struck before a line of pipeline code exists.

**D4 (light mass-form seed) — MEDIUM RISK, with one gift.** The gift: the
fabric conjugate (Φ*, ω→−ω) is an exact equal-mass opposite-charge partner —
the positronium analog needs nothing new (§2.1b). The risk: D4's "prefer fabric
conjugate" cannot produce a *light* partner — conjugation preserves the
spectrum exactly. If Stage 4 needs m_light ≪ m_nucleus even structurally, that
is a new-sector problem the creed does not address; flag it in the creed rather
than letting Stage 4 inherit it silently.

## 6. Kill gates

**G1 — Operationalization gate (after action #1).** If no N-ENT candidate
survives the scaling audit (all degenerate, first-law-trivial, or
cut-dominated), A3 is demoted from definition to heuristic ("entanglement as
mass-form order parameter"), and v84 proceeds on δE + process closure alone. No
pipeline is built for a struck candidate.

**G2 — Redundancy gate (after the pre-registered experiment).** If
N-M = k·δE with universal k across the full triple + branch scan (within the
two-spacing scheme error), the creed is empirically equivalent to E=mc²; retire
A3's division, keep A1/A2/A5/A6, and record the equivalence as a *result*
(monism confirmed in its trivial form), not a failure to be re-litigated.

**G3 — Inertia gate (standing).** If N-M and inertial response disagree beyond
lattice error where both are measurable, N-M is not mass. Rename the quantity
(entanglement inventory), restore inertia/δE as the mass definition, and keep
the inventory only if it predicts something (stability, binding, branch ends).

**G4 — Stage-3 carrier gate.** If the conjugate-pair experiment shows no bound
state at any separation inside the validated medium-Coulomb regime (with closed
ledgers — i.e., not a protocol confound; apply the Fable R2 checklist), the
blocker is the binding sector, not mass ontology, and no amount of creed
refinement addresses it. Escalate to a carrier/sector design discussion with
explicit kernel-authorization scope.

**G5 — Scheme-dependence tripwire.** If N-M *orderings* (not values) flip
between two lattice spacings, the measure is regulator noise; strike it and
return to G1.

## 7. Stop-doing

1. **Stop calling classical proxies "entanglement" unqualified.** Every use of
   N-ENT in documents must name the ensemble (fluctuation-Gaussian,
   cycle-ensemble, …). "Entanglement" without a density matrix is a metaphor,
   and metaphors have already cost this project one advice arc (v83 CLOSEOUT
   §3, last row).
2. **Stop treating the inertia test as optional** (creed §5.4). It is the
   referee — cheap, already designed (Fable R2 F5-style), and the only
   connection between "M" and the word *mass*.
3. **Stop carrying the lattice-causal-cell option in D2.** It is a regulator
   parameter; every week it survives in the creed is a week someone might build
   on it.
4. **Stop Stage-3 carrier engineering until the metrology exists** — no new
   lock variants, no multiplet revivals, no orbit matrices (already in the
   creed's reject list; extend it to *any* new carrier design pre-G1/G2).
5. **Stop implying ħ_eff = 2πQ is the ħ of QFT** in forward-looking text. It is
   a measured adiabatic invariant / candidate process quantum — the creed's own
   hedge (§2.4) is correct; enforce it everywhere else.
6. **Do not re-open v80–v83 failed methods** (creed §4.2) under creed
   vocabulary. A renamed bag is still a bag. Any restart requires a written new
   creed-level premise, per the creed's own rule.
