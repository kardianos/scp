## 1. Creed axioms

### A1 — One continuum
There is one geometric fabric. Free and mass-form are modes of the same continuum. No dualist stage + stuff as ontology.

### A2 — Energy is field delta
\[
\boxed{E \;=\; \delta(\text{field})}
\]

**Meaning:**

- \(E\) measures **how the continuum differs** from a free/vacuum reference arrangement (and/or from a free process ledger), not “energy of a substance on spacetime.”  
- Operationally: \(\delta\) is a **contrast** — configuration and process relative to free continuum (energy density relative to vacuum; uptake/layment imbalance; locked vs free budget).  
- Bulk integrals \(\int T_{00}\) may **estimate** a delta when vacuum is zero and free radiation is subtracted; they are **not** the definition of \(E\) if treated as independent stuff energy.

### A3 — Mass is entangled mass per locality quantum unit
\[
\boxed{M \;=\; \frac{\text{entanglement content of mass-form}}{\text{locality quantum unit}}}
\]

**Meaning:**

- **Locality quantum unit:** the indivisible unit of free-fabric causal structure (the quantum of locality). Related to free update bound \(c\) and to process action quanta (historically \(\hbar_{\mathrm{eff}}=Q\) per clock cycle is a *candidate* locality/process quantum — to be refined, not smuggled as bulk \(\hbar\)).  
- **Entanglement content:** multipartite / multi-mode correlation structure that makes mass-form a single object (not a bag of independent local bits). Mass is **not** mere energy density; it is **how much entangled structure** sits on each locality quantum.  
- \(M\) is intensive/relational in the quantum unit; total inertial content of an object is entanglement inventory over its locality quanta.

### A4 — Relation of \(E\) and \(M\) (not dualist \(E=mc^2\))
Dualist habit: \(E=mc^2\) converts stuff-energy to mass via universal \(c\).

**Creed form:**

- \(c\) (locality) defines the **quantum unit** of free causal structure.  
- \(E=\delta(\text{field})\) is the **energetic contrast** of the arrangement.  
- \(M\) is **entangled structure per locality quantum**.  
- Consistency: locking free continuum into mass-form changes both \(\delta(\text{field})\) and entanglement per locality quantum; free paths at fixed local \(c\) look warped globally.  
- **Not claimed yet:** a closed formula \(M=\delta E/c^2\). That formula is dualist residue unless derived from entanglement density × locality quanta.

### A5 — Process closure
Mass-form persists when fabric **uptake = layment** over a cycle (v73 process-form). Radiation = unclosed layment. Stationary Q-ball envelopes are closed processes, not motionless lumps.

### A6 — Charts are re-partitions
C / M / E charts (causal, mass, energy) are views of one state. Export grids and lock registries are **instruments**, not home ontology unless they implement A2–A3.
