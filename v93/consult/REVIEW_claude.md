# v93 review — the unitary dense channel: theory, code, results, conclusions

**Reviewer summary (most important first):**

1. **Bug (Go kernel): the dense in-pass precession has sin/cos swapped.** `fab/step.go:467` does `cc, ss := math.Sincos(ang)` — Go's `Sincos` returns **(sin, cos)**, so `cc` receives sin(ang). The dense clock in Go rotates by ≈ π/2 − w2e·dt every step instead of w2e·dt. Every other `Sincos` call in the file uses the correct idiom (`ss, cc := math.Sincos(...)` at step.go:486, 1137, 1169). The battery cannot see this (amp_nat=0 skips pass U), which also proves **no C≡Go A/B has ever been run at amp_nat>0** — the "C≡Go" line in the results header of REVIEW_REQUEST.md is true only at amp_nat=0. Secondary: even after the swap fix, C uses the polynomial pair `lut_cos/lut_sin` for this rotation (freecell.c:1546) while Go uses libm `math.Sincos`; mirror C's choice (Go already has `lutCos/lutSin`, used correctly at step.go:454-455).
2. **L1-B and L1-C were graded on a run that contains no dense matter at all.** `exp=bath` seeds nothing (freecell.c:3938 — "exp=bath: nothing to seed"); with wf_on=1 no condensation can ever occur, so Em≡Ee≡0 for the whole run, and every slot exits pass 2 at the mobility early-out (freecell.c:1359) before τ_s is ever computed. The drift-0.000e+00 table and the byte-identity table are **vacuous with respect to the unitary channel**. Worse, the only live-matter drift number in the recorded artifacts — `runs/quench_argdoor.out`, arm h3_uni_clk (amp_nat=2, T=300) — is **drift_rel 2.148e-13, above the registered ≤1e-13 bar** (README III.2). Conservation is still fine physically (see Q1), but the GREEN as published is not what was measured.
3. **The "self-gating" mechanism claim is quantitatively wrong.** For random phases the closure gate is not ≈0: `gate_of` = ((1+cos ψ)/2)^p_gate = cos^{2p}(ψ/2), whose uniform-phase mean at p_gate=8 (freecell.c:363) is C(16,8)/4⁸ ≈ 0.196, and ⟨√(g_ij·g_ji)⟩ ≈ 0.07–0.2. On incoherent dense matter the channel is *active* — zero mean current but strong diffusive scrambling — exactly what the driven-beam arm shows (births 8800→3100–4400, a 2–3× change in the creation law). The true anti-ignition guarantee is unitarity itself (transport cannot raise ΣEm) plus the unchanged door — that argument is sound — but the design's own live bar (§II.9: "creation rate matching the measured quench law when live") has not been run. L1-C should be demoted from GREEN to "structural argument + not yet measured on an armed bath."
4. **A real code artifact both kernels share: pass U's write-back pins th2 of every Em=0 cell to 0 every step** (freecell.c:1576-1582: `atan2(0,0)` = 0; same in step.go:502-510). At amp_nat>0 the entire vacuum/bath medium — every cell the packet must expand into — becomes phase-uniform (th2=0, never advancing, since pass 6's advance is skipped at freecell.c:2143), where the additive world gives those cells random, precessing clocks. Since τ_s depends on phases through g_ij(thi, thj), the blob at amp_nat>0 propagates into an artificially phase-flattened medium. This artifact is plausibly load-bearing on the L1-A coherence window and must be removed (skip the th2 write when Em=0, preserving the old phase) before the window is trusted.
5. **The e3b meter can't distinguish translation from asymmetric melt, and the baseline already "translates."** The centroid is computed over tagged cells only (freecell.c:2543), i.e., the fixed initial cell set; a genuinely translating unitary packet exports amplitude to untagged +x cells, which this meter mostly misses, while a 3–4% one-sided leak of Em_tag (measured melt: 3.6%/42 t.u.) reproduces the entire measured displacement (~0.26 l.u. over T=80) without any packet motion. Moreover `runs/l1_sweep.out` shows the **additive baseline (amp_nat=0) already drifts coherently at the same speed** — seed 111: speed 0.003138, cos −0.94 — so the v93 delta is a *direction flip to +kdir with 5/5 seed robustness*, not new speed; the speed bar (≥2.6e-3) is met by the null mechanism and cannot certify the new one. And |cos|≈1 is near-guaranteed by symmetry (kx is the only x-symmetry breaker), so it measures axis alignment, not ballisticity.
6. **The design's own staged acceptance failed silently.** README III.2 pre-registered (per grok): "median |cos| improves and seed variance *falls* at small amp_nat — not a cliff." The recorded sweep (`runs/l1_sweep.out`, amp_nat 0.25/0.5/1.0) shows the opposite: speeds collapse to 0.0003–0.002 and cos goes incoherent/backward. The findings never mention this. Under the ratchet's own rules that's a bar miss that needs an explicit reckon, not silence.
7. **Provenance/harvest bugs:** `runs/l1_sweep.sh` greps `"RESULT drift_rel|drift" | head -1` and captures the diag **column header**, not a number (visible in l1_sweep.out); `runs/quench_argdoor.out`'s "R2d retention curve" lines are mangled (`R2d=pk=m-3`). The published findings tables cannot be regenerated from the committed artifacts. Fix the harvests, re-run, and re-check the published numbers against them.

None of this kills the thesis: the algebra of pass U is correct and genuinely conservative, the L2 fifth engaging 0→0.132 only under the unitary channel is a real qualitative first, and the direction-robust +kdir window at amp_nat=2.0 across 5 seeds is a real effect *of something*. But between the Go bug, the vacuum-phase artifact, the melt-vs-translation ambiguity, and the vacuous L1-B/L1-C runs, the "CONFIRMED" language in the consolidated verdict is running ahead of the evidence.

## Q1 — Theory/code soundness

**The Givens hop is correct and genuinely unitary.** Writing ψ = m1 + i·m2, the update at freecell.c:1561-1565 is ψ_i′ = cos τ·ψ_i − i·sin τ·ψ_j, ψ_j′ = cos τ·ψ_j − i·sin τ·ψ_i, i.e. U = exp(−iτσ_x): unitary, Hermitian generator, pairwise norm exact up to trig rounding. The per-hop energy transfer is ΔEm_i = sin²τ·(|ψ_j|²−|ψ_i|²) + 2 sin τ cos τ·Im(ψ_i*ψ_j) — to first order the claimed J = 2τ·Im(ψ_i*ψ_j). No sign error. The p1 meter (freecell.c:1566-1573) books actual per-hop ΔEm_j × displacement, which is correct regardless of τ's size, and is your most under-used instrument (see Q2).

**Direction bookkeeping is consistent.** The seed writes th2 = −kx·dx (freecell.c:3452), i.e. mode k = −kx; for U = exp(−iτσ_x) (positive hopping) the group velocity is v = −2J̄d·sin(kd)|_{k=−kx} = +2J̄d·sin(kx·d) > 0 → **+x is the theoretically expected direction**, matching the amp_nat=2.0 window. Corollary: the −x windows at 1.5/2.25 are the anomaly to explain, not the +x one. Second corollary: free-theory speed is linear in amp_nat, and the data agree startlingly well within a seed (seed 111: 0.000266 at amp_nat=0.25 vs 0.002387 at 2.0 — ratio 9.0 vs amp ratio 8). The current mechanism behaves like free tight-binding; it's the *direction/coherence* that misbehaves off-window (see Q2).

**In-pass precession ordering: right as a pass-F mirror, with one caveat.** The precess-then-hop order (freecell.c:1538-1550) plus the pass-6 skip (freecell.c:2143) is a faithful mirror of pass F (2056-2062) and the face-2→3 wobble reduction is believable Trotter physics. The caveat: pass U is **not** actually pass F's cousin in one deep respect — pass F's hop angle is pure geometry (freecell.c:2082), while pass U's τ_s is a function of the phases themselves via √(g_ij·g_ji) (freecell.c:1477-1478, computed in pass 2 from *pre-step* th2). So the dense channel is a *state-dependent* (nonlinear) unitary: conservation still holds for any τ (unitarity doesn't care), but superposition/interference intuitions imported from the field sector do not transfer, and τ(phase) is itself a parametric-drive mechanism (the gates on p≠q links rotate at comb frequencies) that can produce amp_nat windows on its own. The brief's own suspicion here is well-founded; the test is cheap (below).

**Trig accuracy is a non-issue** — I checked: `lut_cos/lut_sin` are not a coarse table but range-reduction + minimax polynomial (v89/int_ledger/trig_lut.inc), ~1-ulp class, so the sqrt→trig→atan2 round trip and non-exactly-unit rotations accumulate drift only at the 1e-16/step level. The measured live drift 2.148e-13 over 15,000 steps (h3_uni_clk) is consistent with exactly that. The theorem is real; the *bar as written* (≤1e-13) is exceeded on the only live measurement, and the published 0.000e+00 comes from the matterless bath. Re-measure on e3b/quench arms and either re-gauge the bar with the user (IV.9) or report per-step-normalized drift.

**The arg(ψ) door: energy-conserving, yes — but it is not the design's §II.7 mechanism, and it is not "coherent."** The code (freecell.c:2178-2193) writes only th2; Em is fixed beforehand, so conservation is trivial. But note three things. (a) The renormalization *discards the interference cross-term in magnitude* (a true coherent merge would give |ψ|² = Em_old + d1 + 2√(Em_old·d1)cos Δ; you keep Em). That's the right conservative choice — but it means the door still "rejects the interference term" exactly as the additive ledger did in-mode: §II.4's resolution does **not** extend across the mode boundary, and the P2/radiation-pressure deficit should not be expected to close from this door alone. (b) For small clicks the door is a *strengthened* qp_phase: effective pull ~√(d1/Em)·sinΔ versus qp_phase's (d1/Em)·Δ — a bigger mix coefficient toward the same phase source (the local, instantaneous field phase), not a categorically different mechanism. The measured signature — 3× longer early-time retention, unchanged steady state — is exactly what a stronger pull toward a decohering source produces: it helps while RA2 is high and *actively scrambles faster* once the arriving field is speckle. (c) Nothing here is "slot-borne": condensation is a purely local event, so §II.7's slot-borne-atom language doesn't even apply to this direction; it applies to matter→field→matter recycling — and there the phase is simply *discarded*: `field_inject` (freecell.c:922-934) adds evaporated energy by magnitude rescale whenever the cell already has field (line 927-928), using th2's phase only into an empty field. The fired atom does **not** carry arg(ψ_m) anywhere today. The §II.7 face is, at best, half-built.

**A convention worth an explicit audit:** the field precesses e^{−i·w1e·dt} (freecell.c:2060-2062) while the dense amplitude precesses e^{+i·w2e·dt} (1544-1550) — opposite senses in both kernels. Any door phase comparison (aph vs th2) between two cells clicking at slightly different times therefore dephases at w1e+w2e (~4.5 rad/t.u.), not at the beat (~1.25). If deliberate, document it; if not, it's a large, free retention lever (and it interacts with (b) above).

**Minor:** the door's phasor uses ro=√Em_old, rc=√d1, while the energy actually added is d1+dsp — the space-pulled share is phase-orphaned (freecell.c:2188-2192); harmless but should be stated. The τ clamp at 0.5 (freecell.c:1479) is arbitrary and, by my estimate (base = kd·dt·geo·gpl·res ≲ 0.25 with kd=2.4, dt=0.02 — freecell.c:364, 392), the strongest unison links *start hitting it right around amp_nat≈2* — see Q2. Sequential non-commuting hops in roster index order (freecell.c:1551-1575) add an O(τ²) ordered-product bias whose orientation is set by the dart-throw order, i.e. by the seed — a candidate seed-variance source. And pass U lacks pass F's Hodge normalization (pass F: τ ∝ w/√(s_i·s_j), freecell.c:2082; pass U: raw base·gsym) — on a foam with z ≈ 12–20 this makes J̄ vary ~1.7× cell-to-cell, i.e. built-in dispersion disorder, another seed-variance candidate with a principled fix.

## Q2 — The narrow resonance: real dispersion or artifact?

My read: **the window is real but composite** — a low edge that is probably mundane, a high edge that is probably an artifact family, and a genuinely under-determined middle. Four candidate mechanisms, each with a cheap discriminating test:

1. **Crossover against the door-recycling drift (low edge).** At amp_nat=0.25 the blob still moves coherently *backward* (l1_sweep.out: −0.95 at 0.000266) even though the additive dense want is off — so there is an amp_nat-independent −x transport channel, almost certainly the matter→field→matter recycling loop (evap → field_inject → pass F → recondense on wf-qualifying blob cells), which is also the obvious explanation of the unexplained *baseline* −x drift at amp_nat=0. The unitary +x current (linear in amp_nat) overtakes it near amp_nat 1.5–2 — the "window onset" may be nothing more than this crossover. **Test:** run e3b with `p1_meter=1` and read the channel-resolved COE flows (the apparatus exists — freecell.c:327); and/or an f_conv=0 probe arm to kill recycling. This decomposition is one run away and should precede any theory of the window.
2. **Bloch oscillation under the self-generated detune gradient (the wobble, and possibly the reversals).** The in-pass precession uses load-detuned w2e = w2/(1+q_detune·x) (freecell.c:1311-1314), so the blob's own load profile is a potential slope ΔΩ ~ O(1) across σ=2.5 — a constant force F ~ 0.4 on a lattice, Bloch period 2π/(F·d) ~ 13–20 t.u., matching the measured wobble (com_dev 0.06→0.10→0.07 over ~40 t.u.). Under Bloch dynamics the *time-averaged* direction depends on how many periods fit in T=80 as amp_nat scales the band — alternating windows are natural. **Tests:** time-resolved v(t) from the existing centroid traces (count oscillations); the q_detune sweep — already started (`runs/face4_qdetune.out` has only the q=1.2 block; the q=0.6/0.3/0.0 arms are the decisive ones, and it lacks a `.done`, so do not cite it yet).
3. **Floquet/per-step-angle aliasing and the τ clamp (high edge).** τ and the precession are per-step angles ∝ dt; if the windows live in absolute per-step rotation (aliasing) they shift under dt-halving, whereas a genuine rate-ratio resonance (J̄ vs Ω — both dt-independent) stays at amp_nat≈2. **Test:** rerun the fine sweep at dt=0.01, T unchanged; also rerun with the clamp at 0.25 and 1.0 (freecell.c:1479) — if the window tracks the clamp, it's the clamp; log the τ_s distribution either way.
4. **The parametric τ(phase) coupling** (the brief's own suspicion): **test** by a probe knob that freezes τ_s at its t=0 values (or replaces gsym with its blob-average constant). Window survives → dispersion/aliasing; window dies or widens → the parametric coupling is the driver.

Before any of that, though, do the two hygiene items: fix the th2=0 vacuum pinning (summary #4) and re-run the fine sweep — a phase-flattened medium is a plausible *cause* of a coherence window — and switch the meter (summary #5): report all-cell Em first moment (blob2 already computes one — freecell.c:4068-4072), the p1fd integrated current, and the Em(x) profile over time. A translating packet moves its *peak*; an asymmetric melt erodes an edge under a stationary peak. At current numbers the blob half-melts (~600 t.u. at 3.6%/42) before it could move its own width (~800 t.u. at 0.003), so a decisive version of L1-A needs either longer-lived packets or a displacement-based bar (≥2σ) rather than a fitted speed that the null mechanism already clears.

**To widen/pin seed-robustly** (ranked): (i) add pass F's √(s_i·s_j) normalization to τ_s — principled (it's the Hodge star the synthesis already blessed, SUBQUARK_synthesis.md), homogenizes J̄ across coordination disorder, directly aimed at the seed-variant |cos|; (ii) the q_detune=0 result will tell you whether detune shear is the variance source (the findings' own suspicion — I agree, with the Bloch mechanism as the concrete form); (iii) consider a Peierls phase on the hop (τ → complex with link phase w̄·d/C): the unitary channel silently dropped the flight-retardation physics the additive channel carried (slph advance, and the gates still assume it — freecell.c:1380-1381); restoring it inside unitarity is the natural next face and the natural home of "bed"/curvature later; (iv) reverse the link application order as a one-line probe for the ordered-product bias.

## Q3 — The QUENCH-3 retention wall

The churn diagnosis is *plausible but not established*, because three walls operate on the same timescale and the experiment as run cannot separate them: (a) **field transit decoherence** — RA2 1.0→0.15; note the v91 HORIZON measurement that the foam is optically thick at ~20 cells: the vortex arrives pre-scrambled, and *no door can retain what does not arrive*; (b) **matter churn** — h3_uni_clk: NC=2755, births 33581 / deaths 33911 over T=300 (runs/quench/h3_uni_clk.log), mean cell lifetime ≈ NC·T/deaths ≈ 24 t.u., matching the R2d decay scale; and (c) **rewrite-by-speckle** — unlisted in the findings, and to me the most likely proximate killer: condensation *continues* throughout the run, and every later click coherently merges the now-decohered local field phase into th2; the arg-door's stronger pull makes this *worse* late exactly as much as it helps early — which is precisely the measured signature. Also note the steady-state R2d ≈ 0.02–0.03 is the 1/√N random floor for N~10³ contributing cells, i.e. genuinely zero retention, and newborn cells get th2 pinned to 0 by the pass-U artifact — every rebirth is a guaranteed phase-slip site under the current code.

**Distinguishing experiments, in order:** (1) **Direct dense imprint** — seed th2 = 2φ on an existing matter ring/cloud at t0, no beam, no door writes: measures pure matter-side retention (unitary transport + churn) with zero field involvement; this is the single cleanest fork and hasn't been run. (2) **Door-freeze retention:** transient beam, then f_conv=0 (or wf_far→∞) from t0 — if R2d then persists to the churn timescale, (c) was the killer; if it still dies in ~10 t.u., churn/transport is. (3) **Age-conditioned R2d** (cells older than the imprint) separates churn from in-place decoherence. (4) **Near-source imprint** (source adjacent to the cloud) bounds (a). Code fixes that could yield real steady-state retention if (c)/(artifact) dominate: stop pinning newborn/empty th2; gate door writes by local field coherence (only merge when the local RA2-proxy is high — a lawful "the door believes coherent light only" rule worth pre-registering); and audit the counter-rotating door frames (Q1). If (a) dominates, retention is bounded upstream by pass-F disorder physics and the honest statement is that the *medium*, not the door, forbids field-carried winding at this range.

## Q4 — Packet binding

A linear unitary hop cannot bind — that is not a defect of the implementation but a theorem of the channel class; the additive model's binding came from cap/headroom/inflight, which face 2 deliberately dropped. Two directions, not exclusive:

- **Reframe L1-A for this lane** (recommended): the natural acceptance for a *linear* coherent channel is wave-packet transport — group velocity of the all-cell Em first moment matching 2J̄d·sin(kx·d), sustained integrated current p1fd, dispersion σ(t) consistent with band curvature — with "bound translating object" split off as a separate, later bar. This is not softening (IV.9); it is measuring the right observable for the mechanism actually built, and it converts the seed-variance problem into a quantitative dispersion measurement.
- **The binding companion may already be in the kernel.** Pass U + load-detuned precession w2e(Em) *is* a discrete NLS with a saturable on-site self-frequency shift — the standard soliton-former. Whether it focuses or defocuses depends on the sign of the band curvature at the packet's k relative to the detune sign; ε(k) = 2J̄Σcos(kd) has both curvatures across the band, so a self-focusing k-window plausibly exists. Concrete probe: sweep kx toward the band edge (kx·d → π) at amp_nat=2 and look for melt-rate suppression. If found, "packet binding" costs zero new physics — the same term currently blamed for shear becomes the binder in the right window (dispersion management, in fiber-optics terms).
