## 2. Verdicts on H1–H4

### H1 — "2B is required to unblock Stage 3": DISAGREE (strong form), PARTIAL (weak form)

- **Strong form is wrong on the program's own definitions.** Stage 3 = light
  opposite-charge stable solitons, positronium analog first (CLAUDE.md, CARBON_MAP).
  Positronium contains no nucleus. Its acceptance test (Q8, §3.8 below) runs
  entirely in the light sector. Stage 3 is blocked by light-sector physics — live
  PIC fragility, self-force, 2D-vs-3D — not by nuclear anatomy.
- **The Stage-4 version ("a droplet is an insufficient attachment target") is a
  hypothesis, not a fact, and it is cheap to falsify.** In real physics the electron
  cloud couples to the nucleus through its monopole charge plus tiny corrections;
  chemistry is famously insensitive to nuclear substructure. If the parked Z-carbon
  droplet (Q≈650, on-branch) presents a clean exterior Coulomb field to a light
  lock, and the lock can orbit it, the strong form of H1 is dead and Stage 4 is
  open. That experiment (E1) uses only existing assets: the shared Gauss law
  ∇·E = g(ρ_matter + ρ_lock) is already in the kernel (KERNEL_PORT.md).
- What survives of H1: "the light sector must be able to *couple to* the nucleus"
  (gap #5). That is a coupling/interface question, not a multi-center-anatomy
  question. Do not let the two masquerade as each other.

### H2 — "high lock : single anti-lock core": UNPROVEN; three structural objections

1. **By-construction success.** The bag force is a hand-inserted attractive
   potential. A cluster of soft-core charge locks around one attractor is a
   Thomson-problem toy; it will park for some (κ, r_bag). A park therefore
   demonstrates parameter existence, not fabric necessity. Any PASS must be
   accompanied by an N_anti=0 control (soft core + medium alone) and by a
   perturbation test, or it is decoration.
2. **Adverse stoichiometric scaling.** N same-sign charge locks repel pairwise via
   the medium: repulsion energy ~ N². One anti-lock supplies ~N bag bonds. Unless κ
   is scaled with N (tuning smell), there is an N_max above which the unit
   evaporates locks — you will re-derive Q_max in a toy registry. Interesting as a
   rhyme, but not a new capability. The sweep should *measure* N_max(κ, r_bag)
   rather than assume "high lock count" is reachable.
3. **Open ledger.** An anti-lock has q=0 and no ρ/J; if the bag force does not
   back-react on a tracked energy reservoir, the unit's binding energy is paid from
   nowhere. That violates the program's own process-form standards (v73). Kill
   gate: total-energy drift and a defined E* bookkeeping for the anti, *before*
   trusting any park.

Also: the motivating analogy is weak. im2's "single frustrated channel" is the
anatomy of an object **in the process of decaying** (−33%/500 t.u.). Restating a
decay mode in a different substrate as a design target imports the geometry without
the mechanism.

Prior I'd assign: ≤40% that a 1-anti unit parks at N_lock ≥ 6 without κ(N) tuning;
and even conditional on a park, unclear that anything downstream consumes it.

### H3 — ordering: DISAGREE; invert it

Unit → multi-center carbon → exterior light serializes Stage 3 behind two unbuilt
speculative constructions. Order by information per day instead: the exterior-attach
test (E1) is first because it uses existing parked assets, tests H1 directly, and
its *failure modes are as informative as its success* (see §4). The unit build is a
parallel toy-registry sweep, not a gate on the light sector.

### H4 — falsifiers: right spirit, incomplete list

Grok's falsifiers are good (especially "pure gauged interlock works → anti-lock
unnecessary" — run exactly that, E2). Add:

- **Droplet-attach falsifier:** a light lock orbits parked c6_light with honest
  monopole bookkeeping → H1 strong form dead. (Grok lists a weaker version gated on
  free-pair robustness; it should not be gated — run it now.)
- **N_anti=0 control:** unit parks equally well with zero antis → H2 dead.
- **Ledger falsifier:** bag-bound unit shows unaccounted energy → H2 implementation
  is not physics yet.

## 3. Answers to Q1–Q8

### Q1 — Is 2B required, or can droplet 2A + free-pair Stage 3 deliver the atom?

**Droplet + free light sector is the null hypothesis and it has not been tested.**
Until E1 (light lock vs. parked droplet) runs, claiming the droplet is insufficient
is theory taste, not measurement. The physical analog argues *for* the droplet: to
an orbiting electron the nucleus is a point charge; multipole corrections are
(r_nuc/r_orbit)^k-suppressed. The fabric droplet additionally retains flavor
partition and internal clocks through fusion (fn1/fn2) — it already carries the
conserved bookkeeping Stage 4 needs (Q, Q_a, ω_a, Gauss flux). My verdict: 2B is
legitimate *nuclear research* (fission channels, isotope structure, GDR
spectroscopy) and the user wants it worked — fine — but it is **not on the Stage 3
critical path**, and putting it there delays the one sector that is actually
blocking atoms.

One important caveat that could rescue a *reframed* 2B: if E1 fails by
**absorption** (the light lock spirals in and is eaten by the droplet), then a
short-range scale at the nucleus–electron interface is a demonstrated need, and
bag/capacity physics has a real, motivated job there. That would be 2B-adjacent
work justified by measurement — the right way for it to enter the critical path.

### Q2 — Is high-lock : 1-anti the right stoichiometry? Better pins?

See H2 verdict for the three objections. Ranked alternatives for "the pin":

1. **No pin — dynamical binding.** The only mechanism with a PASS on the board.
   For the atom: orbits. For the nucleus: the droplet, which is already parked.
2. **Capacity co-field (v82 A F_core), generalized to N bodies.** Already validated
   as a pair law; declared as the theory's deliberate second-scale postulate; has a
   path to monist semantics (depletion/sequestration) rather than staying a
   registry hack. If any short-range scale gets promoted to "physics," it should be
   this one, once, coherently — not a zoo of bags, wells, and antis.
3. **Gauged Cosserat interlock (FUTURE #11).** Never run; cheap; uses mature
   machinery; success would obviate the anti-lock entirely (Grok's own falsifier).
   Honest prior is low (~25–30%): the linear standoff condition for
   F = C/D² − A e^{−μD} requires μ < 2/D* at the stable root; with μ≈0.48 (ω=1.42)
   that pushes D* below ~4 — inside the nonlinear overlap where tail models already
   failed (im1). But the nonlinear regime is exactly what's unmeasured, and either
   answer permanently closes a cell that keeps getting cited.
4. **Anti-lock bag.** Weakest: hand force, open ledger, adverse N² scaling, and its
   only two prior uses (free-pair bag, S1 grid bag) both failed.

On the ratio itself: if antis are tested at all, **the interesting variable is not
the ratio but the scaling** — sweep N_anti ∈ {0, 1, 2} as controls and measure
N_max(κ). "Exactly one" has no derivation; it is an aesthetic import from im2.

### Q3 — Is im2's cyan composite the right geometric target?

**Red herring.** im2 is the theory's own demonstration that phase frustration is
*not* load-bearing at equilibrium — the cyan anatomy is a decay in progress
(frustrated flavor −33%/500 t.u., clock pushed off-window), and under collision the
interlock survives only as momentum (mc1/mc2 thrust), never as geometry. Rebuilding
that anatomy in the lock registry copies the corpse, not the organism. The single
legitimate descendant of im2 is its **gauged** version (E2), because Coulomb changes
the force budget and was never tested. Do not otherwise design toward cyan.

### Q4 — Substrate for 2B: (A) monist locks, (B) Cosserat + gauge, (C) hybrid?

- **(B) first, as a cheap kill, not a build:** the gauged interlock force map (E2)
  is one GPU campaign on mature tools. If it parks, 2B exists in the native nuclear
  substrate and the whole anti-lock discussion collapses.
- **(A) second, explicitly labeled a toy scaffold:** the unit sweep (E3) is force-law
  discovery in a registry, not a deliverable nucleus. Flag the **ontology fork**

  carriers. A lock-built "carbon center" would mean two competing nuclear

  hybrid *simulation* (both in one kernel, shared Gauss — already implemented),
  which requires no hybrid *object*.
- **(C) hybrid bag-on-Cosserat: defer.** Gap #5 (coupling design) has no spec, and
  hybrid work before both halves park separately is integration risk with no

### Q7 — Biggest cargo-cult risk

**Two-layer cargo cult, and the second layer is worse than the first.**

- *Layer 1 — "a nucleus has parts."* Imported from real physics without fabric
  necessity. In-fabric, necessity of parts is established at the **component**
  level (product potential forces triples; v71 QUARK) — and those parts
  demonstrably survive fusion (flavor retention, GDR). No measured result requires
  retained *ball centers*. Worse for the analogy: real nuclei are themselves
  droplet-like (liquid-drop model works; cluster states are marginal), and real
  atoms couple to nuclei as point charges. So the analogy being imported does not
  even hold in the source domain.
- *Layer 2 — "the pin makes it real."* A park produced by a hand-coded κ∇B force
  with an open energy ledger is indistinguishable from a screensaver. Success is
  chosen by parameters, and every knob (κ, r_bag, soft core) is a place for the
  conclusion to hide.

**Guard rails:** (i) every 2B PASS ships with an N_anti=0 / force-off negative
control; (ii) every 2B PASS ships with a closed ledger; (iii) before spending
further, someone must name the **Stage-4 observable that actually consumes
multi-center anatomy** — a measurement that would come out differently for a
droplet vs. a multi-center nucleus with the same Q and exterior field. If no such
observable can be named, H1 is unfalsifiable decoration and should be reclassified
as taste. (My candidate list is short and telling: hyperfine-analog splitting from
nuclear multipoles, GDR-coupled orbital shifts. Both are *corrections*, not
existence conditions, for an atom.)

### Q8 — Minimal positronium-analog acceptance test (Stage 3 definition)

Pre-register this now, independent of 2B (it needs no nucleus):

**Substrate:** monist locks on live medium — full PIC (Boris + charge-conserving J +
Gauss), *no analytic forces*, no nucleus. Capacity well active, declared in the
theory docs as the light sector's postulated second scale (a physics commitment,
not a per-run tuning).

**PASS requires all of:**
1. **Bound multi-rev:** ≥5 revolutions; separation within [0.7, 1.4]·r_c for the
   whole run; survives ≥10 orbital periods.
2. **Robustness:** stays bound under ±10% seed-velocity perturbation (kills
   knife-edge orbits).
3. **Ledger closed:** Gauss at machine floor; total energy drift bounded with
   radiated E_em accounted; E* bookkeeping exact.
4. **Negative control:** identical seed with the capacity well off must *not* park
   (proves the second scale is load-bearing, not an integrator artifact).
5. **Dimensionality honesty:** the acceptance run is **3D Coulomb**, or is
   explicitly labeled a development result. This matters: the current 2D
   F = 1/(2πD) integrates to a log potential, in which *every* pair is formally
   bound — 2D multi-rev is systematically flattered and cannot certify Stage 3
   (gap #6 resolved by decision, here).

Annihilation is not modeled and not required; "positronium analog" is structural —
a durable bound ± pair with closed books. That is the whole Stage-3 gate. Note it
contains no 2B dependency anywhere.
