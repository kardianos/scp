# v80 DUAL_CHARTS — Real + point of view

## Motivation

User prompt: treat reality as **both real and a point of view**.  
If \(M\) held const in some weird way, would \(c\) vary and space not warp?  
If \(E\) held const, would \(c\) vary and space not warp?  

These are **ledger gauges** on \(E \sim M c^2\), plus a choice of geometric language — not automatically physical free-vacuum laws.

## Three charts

| Chart | Hold | Move | Geometric speech |
|-------|------|------|------------------|
| **C** | free \(c\) | \(\ell\) / free structure; lock \(E,M\) | “Space (measure) warps”; preferred monist free frame (v76) |
| **M** | lock mass *label* | \(c_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and/or free energy table | “Flat stage + variable free speed” (optical dual of path cost) |
| **E** | region energy *budget* | \(M_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and/or \(c_{\mathrm{eff}}\) | “Inertia labels float”; teaches open-system radiation |

Only two of \((E_\star, M_\star, c_\star)\) free given \(E_\star = M_\star c_\star^2\) (schematic).
