## ROUNDS 1-2 (shear kick eps=0.15 on the vacuum cavity)

0.227 frozen, then CREEPS UP in step events. Deformation is permanent.
R2 (kick at t=200 with all 6 links alive): the kick KILLS the network —
links 6 -> 4 within 5 t.u., 2 by t=215, 0 by t=305; strain never
relaxes. Fabric objects under V2g(+rad) are BRITTLE-PLASTIC: no elastic
modes, no QNMs; moderate strain = fracture (pad 04's thermal shock,
observed directly). Bonus: the cavity's links naturally die/re-form over
its life (the R2-campaign "kept all 6 links" was an end-snapshot).
R3: kick the retuned sub-ceiling ring (x=0.50, k=0.13) when alive —
does a balance-fed object acquire any restoring response?

## ROUND 3 (fracture threshold)

~natural flicker level), 0.10 -> 0, 0.15 -> 0 fast (R2). Fracture

then shatter. Brittle-plastic verdict stands, now with a number.
