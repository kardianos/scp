# 03 — a minimum mass for matter (root-N quiets the jitter)
seed: one radiance atom kicks a cell by ~10% of holdings at x*; the 6-cell
ring's gates could not hold through it.
leap: collective modes average fluctuations as 1/sqrt(N). There is an N_min
above which an object at the fixed point is QUIET enough for its bond locks
to survive — a minimum stable mass, like the hydrogen-burning limit. Below
N_min: only bond-free cavities (brown dwarfs); above: true objects (stars).
Size selection has TWO rails: the rung ladder AND the jitter statistics.
ref: minimum main-sequence mass (~0.08 M_sun); fluctuation-threshold
transitions.
first probe: vacuum rings at x*, nv = 6/12/24/48: gate-survival time vs nv.
Cheap (vacuum runs are seconds). Look for the threshold knee.

## ROUND 1 (M5: rings at x*=0.62, vacuum, T=20000, nv sweep)
Edges alive at end: nv=6: 0/6; nv=12: 0/12; nv=24: 0/24; nv=48: 30/48.
A SHARP survival threshold between N=24 and N=48 — the minimum mass for
holding bonded structure at the fixed point exists (first prediction of
this pad confirmed in direction). Energy retention ~0.89-0.91 at every
nv (the cavity leak is N-blind); STRUCTURE survival is the N-gated
thing. Threshold sharper than sqrt-N averaging alone suggests a
cooperative/curvature component (nv=48 bond angle 172.5 deg vs 120 at
nv=6). R2: nv=36 + a 48-cell OPEN CHAIN (curvature control) to locate
the knee and separate geometry from statistics. R3: same ladder at
x=0.50 (sub-ceiling) — does the threshold drop?

## ROUND 2 (nv=36 + the x-control) — THE CURVATURE REFRAME
nv=36@0.62: 17/36 alive (47%) — the turn-on is GRADED (0% at 24, 47%
at 36, 62% at 48). Control nv=48@x=0.50 (sub-ceiling, far less jitter):
32/48 (67%) ~ same as at 0.62 — survival is nearly x-BLIND. That
argues AGAINST jitter-averaging and FOR geometry: bond angle 165 deg
(nv=24, dies) vs 170 (nv=36, half-lives) vs 172.5 (nv=48): a ~5-degree
angular tolerance threshold near 168 deg. The "minimum mass" is really
MINIMUM STRAIGHTNESS — small rings are too curved for the gates.
Supporting: pairs (trivially straight) live at 0.325 indefinitely.
Believer prediction for R3: any near-straight topology of ANY size
lives (needs a chain seeder — apparatus request); sizes are selected
by curvature tolerance, not statistics.
