# 04 — the Biot number of a fabric object (death by thermal shock)
seed: bonded objects out-radiate the single-cell law 2.3x via the Em pile;
the pile is shaved into the bath, never redistributed to the drained cells.
leap: objects die because internal conduction is slower than surface
emission: Biot > 1 — death by thermal shock, like quenched ceramics. Stable
matter must be isothermal (Bi << 1). S2 currents ARE the missing internal
conduction; a bond-conductance dial is the crude alternative. Every R2
corpse should have measured Bi > 1; the surviving 0.325 cavity Bi < 1.
ref: Biot number; thermal-shock fracture criteria.
first probe: from EXISTING R2 logs: inter-cell Em variance growth rate vs
bond transfer rate — tabulate Bi per object, check the live/die split.
