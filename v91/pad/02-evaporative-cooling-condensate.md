# 02 — evaporative cooling INTO the condensate
seed: pile-then-radiate pump-out: the hottest cells' content leaves first;
rings crash BELOW x* (overshoot).
leap: that is evaporative cooling — the lab recipe for making a BEC. Radiance
is the cooling stage the substrate never had; the S2 coherent mode is the
condensate to cool INTO. Run both: a hot object sheds its incoherent fraction
and what remains condenses into the one phase-ordered state — the particle is
what is LEFT when a blob finishes evaporating. Death of the hoard and birth
of the particle are the same process.
ref: evaporative cooling to BEC (Cornell/Wieman/Ketterle); atom laser
outcoupling.
first probe: S2-era: quench an over-full object; plot phase-order parameter
vs falling Em. Prediction: order RISES as mass leaves.
