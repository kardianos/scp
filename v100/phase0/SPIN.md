# Phase 0 — spin level (G2)

**Written:** 2026-09-21.
**Uses the profile:** no. This note is the derivation G2 asked for.

---

## 1. What a pass is

One odd integer \(k\), and a phase \((-1)^k = -1\) for a \(2\pi\)
rotation of a degree-1 frame, obtained from the premises in
`PREREG.md` §3 and from nothing else.

---

## 2. What the frame supplies

A link carries two transverse planes and a relative phase
(`v89/PRINCIPLE.md` §3). That is a rotor. The simply connected
cover of the rotor is \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\). Maps from compactified
space into that cover have a degree \(W\in\mathbb{Z}\). Degree 0
can unwind. Degree 1 cannot, by a homotopy that stays inside the
cover.

\(\pi_4(\mathrm{SU}(2)) = \mathbb{Z}_2\). In the degree-1 sector a
\(2\pi\) rotation is a closed loop, and the loop squares to a
contractible one. Quantization may send that loop to \(+1\) or to
\(-1\). Both are representations of the same fundamental group.
The existence of the nontrivial representation is the statement
that a fermion is allowed. It is not the statement that the
theory uses it.

Finkelstein–Rubinstein identify that rotation loop with an
exchange. Spin and statistics then match, once a representation
is chosen. The identification does not make the choice.

---

## 3. What the two-atom credit supplies

\(\varepsilon = A_0\omega/2\pi\) is an energy. The two-atom credit
says a conversion that fires books two of those quanta. The thing
being counted is a completed cycle of the clock: a door event with
a time period \(2\pi/\omega\).

A \(2\pi\) spatial rotation of a finished texture does not fire
that door. The books of the credit are unchanged if the texture is
rotated and put back. The credit therefore assigns the rotation
neither an energy nor a phase.

The integer in the Wess–Zumino term is not an energy. It pairs a
closed five-chain with the volume form on the target, and that
pairing multiplies the amplitude of the rotation by \((-1)^k\).
Nothing in the credit is a five-chain.

## 5. Verdict

No odd level is fixed. **G2 fails.**

Handedness of the frame remains the sign of the degree,
\(W = \pm 1\). That sign is not spin \(1/2\). Asserting spin
\(1/2\) of this lock would be an imported discrete choice. The
unfold row stays open. This note does not soften the advance rule.
