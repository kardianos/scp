# Design review: the two-component dense parcel

**Bottom line first.** Complexifying the dense parcel is *not* a relabeling — but only if the thing that changes is the **transport algebra** (unitary pairwise rotations replace the additive magnitude want), not the state layout (two floats where one float and an angle used to be). The kernel already *stores* the polar form of a complex number per cell (`fa1 = √E·cos th2`, `fa2 = √E·sin th2` at `freecell.c:903`) and already *stores* a complex shadow per slot-direction (`sre_/sim_`). What it lacks is composition-as-transport. Of your three geometries: **(a) projective is pure notation, (b) the torus is real physics but at the parcel level, not the cell level, and (c) DEC is the strongest — it is the language in which your two hardest recurring failures (the zero-sum renorm patch, conservation under a dying roster) become theorems instead of hand-built fixes.** The uptake/outtake reading is correct physics attached to the wrong object: it describes the link current and the doors, not the Re/Im pair.

## 1. Uptake/outtake as the two components — wrong slot, and actively dangerous

The two components of a complex amplitude are a **frame, not two things**. Under the local clock, `th2` advances at the cell's pitch, so m1 and m2 sweep into each other every fraction of a beat — the same U(1) covariance the field's two planes have. The field sector never assigns physical jobs to fa1 vs fa2, and that is not an accident: any law that reads m1 *as* intake samples a rotating frame, i.e. couples stroboscopically at the pitch. That is a **parametric pump** — a lock-in that rewards whatever population is phase-aligned with the readout. Your bath's unison coherence is measured at ρ_coh ≈ 0.77 (the Phase-M inversion grok flagged as the design's weakest point), so a component-asymmetric coupling is precisely **ignition-shaped**: it hands the m=1 bath a rectifier. And a component-asymmetric law with per-component dynamics is the first step back down the flavored-quark road — components with independent identities is exactly what the pre-v89 ban is about. So the direct answer to your question: **if uptake/outtake stays interpretation, it changes nothing; if it changes the math, it changes it in the harmful direction.** "Individually incomplete sub-components" is right, but the correct sense of incomplete is *gauge-covariant* — and that is exactly why they cannot carry separate physical jobs.

Where uptake/outtake **is** real in your own record, three places, none of them the component pair:

- **The link current and its moments.** With ψ on cells and rotation hops, the current is the cross term J_s = Im(ψᵢ*·w·ψⱼ) per link. Around a cell, the **monopole** of J (net δJ) is metabolism — eat vs shine; the **dipole** (first moment) is momentum. That is your own v89 law — "momentum as the first moment of conversion" — landing exactly where it should. A mover is a cell/parcel with balanced monopole and nonzero dipole: uptake fore, outtake aft, as a *configuration of one current field*, not as two state components.
- **The doors.** The charter itself already registers object-level uptake/outtake as the gravity acceptance: "an amplitude current with spatially separated intake and exhaust — a chain that eats at one end and shines at the other" (README, open-books + beds). Intake = the conversion/space-sector door (the g1 footprint side), outtake = the radiance channel. These are **mode boundaries**, with the in-mode amplitude current running between them. Three-part anatomy: intake door → in-mode current → outtake door.
- **The measured metabolic doublet.** ASTRO's eat-at-U/shine-at-D is a two-*mode* structure (two comb lines), not a two-plane structure. If you genuinely want the two sub-components to *be* uptake and outtake, they must be two **pitches** (a U-mode amplitude and a D-mode amplitude — two complex components, CP¹, see 3a) — which is L-2/L-3 territory, not the minimal complexification.

## 3. The three geometries

**(a) Projective line — pure notation. Flag it.** [m1 : m2] with real homogeneous coordinates is RP¹ ≅ S¹ — literally the phase circle. "Magnitude on the affine line, shape on the projective line" is the polar decomposition (Em, th2) **the kernel already stores**. Projectivizing adds no mechanism, no conservation structure, no new observable. The one honest nearby object: with two *complex* components (the U/D mode doublet of §1), the projective space is CP¹ — the Bloch sphere — and then it is genuinely new structure (internal state = where the parcel sits in its metabolic cycle, total phase factored out by the Hopf fibration). But that is a 4-real-component cell and a bigger campaign. As proposed (2 real components): relabeling.

**(b) Torus — real content, but the torus is the parcel's shape, not the cell's.** A single complex cell has one phase — one S¹. To hang T² on a *cell* you need two phases (the doublet again). But at the **parcel** level the torus is free and matches your record precisely: a ring parcel (your persistent matter *is* rings — nv=48, nv=6, comp12) carries phase winding n around the ring cycle × the clock cycle, and a *moving* ring is a helix on that torus. The physics content: **winding is topologically protected iff |ψ| > 0 along the cycle, and every conversion event is an amplitude zero-crossing — a phase-slip site.** That single sentence retrodicts both halves of your winding record: exact closure lives longest (comp12 ring_m=5, v89) while winding dies in transit at the doors (τ_φ ≈ 3, QUENCH-3 retention zero, CO-RL winding wall not generalizing). It also makes the README's registered spin acceptance ("the m=+2 texture must outlive its driving field") exactly the right bar: it is a phase-slip suppression measurement. New predictions it generates: circulation on an N-cell ring quantized in steps of 2π/N (measurable), and — speculative, flag it as such — a mover's speed as the helical ratio of transport-winding advance to clock advance, which the p:q chart lock would prefer at rational ratios: a **velocity comb**. Test against the e3b speed distribution someday; don't build for it now. Verdict: keep the torus as the *acceptance-metric language* (winding retention, slip rates at doors), not as per-cell structure.

**(c) DEC — the strongest, and the closest to what you already secretly do.** The mapping is exact, not analogy: cells = 0-cells, slots = 1-cells; ψ a 0-cochain; wants/in-flight/currents 1-cochains; d = the along-link difference (the gradient a want should read); δ = the signed incident sum (net arrival). The grok-required "signed zero-sum current on the per-slot shadow" *is* the statement "J is a 1-cochain."
2. **Dynamical roster is DEC's home turf, not its weakness.** DEC is combinatorial — incidence relations, no coordinates — which is background-free in exactly your sense. Cell birth/death = the complex changing; conservation degrades gracefully to boundary flux at doors and roster events. Caveats: DEC is bookkeeping, not physics — it decides none of retention, anti-ignition, or nucleation. As **operators**, not components.

## 4. The big question: what makes this more than "complexify Em"

**The algebra, and only the algebra.** The scalar want lives in the positive reals — a semiring with no cancellation. Unitary composition buys three capabilities that no positive-transport law can express, and each maps to a measured absence:

1. **Destructive interference of transport.** Translation *is* self-interference — constructive fore, destructive aft. A magnitude want cannot cancel; that is *why* three feedback forms couldn't manufacture coherence (a bias can redistribute positives; it cannot make two paths subtract). This is the e3b/L1-A absence.
2. **Sub-threshold reachability.** Rotations move amplitude through dead cells without any live-gate prerequisite; gates move from links to doors ("amplitudes within a mode, atoms at mode boundaries" — the dense sector finally obeying your own v89 synthesis, which the gated want currently violates in spirit). This is the dead-fifth absence (carrier half).
3. **A carried first moment.** The phase gradient is momentum *stored in the state*, not a diagnostic re-derived from centroid drift. This is the P1/P2/B2 absence.

And the conservation crux — |ΣA|² ≠ Σ|A|² — **dissolves rather than gets solved**, which is the signature of the right formulation: with pairwise Givens hops you never sum amplitudes and then square. Each rotation conserves the two-cell norm exactly (this is precisely how the field sector is unitary to roundoff), and the cross term 2·Re(ψᵢ*wψⱼ) that breaks additive bookkeeping **is the link current — it is where momentum lives**. The failed feedbacks kept additive Em-conservation and bolted phase alongside; the cross term had nowhere to live, so it surfaced first as throughput amplification (xsec over-absorption) and then had to be renormed away (zero-sum), which degraded the very signal you wanted (e3b |cos| 0.28→0.08). That sequence is not bad luck — it is the additive ledger rejecting the interference term. Conservation must become a *theorem of the update* (norm preservation), not a ledger patched per form.

Against the three standing constraints:

- **Conservation:** strictly better than all feedback forms — exact by unitarity, no renorm, drift at floor by construction. Doors remain the only places energy changes mode, unchanged.
- **Anti-ignition:** *mixed, and this is the honest risk.* Unitarity kills the entire energy-runaway class outright (transport cannot amplify). Coherence runaway — grok's named risk — remains, and the measured bath ρ_coh 0.766 is the armed threat. One structural point in your favor: the current kernel's `kappa_lock` term (`freecell.c:1533`) is a dissipative Kuramoto-style phase *pull*, and phase-pulling locks are precisely your four measured ignition machines; rotations don't pull phases, they exchange amplitude — reversibly, with no fixed point at alignment. Replacing pull with composition arguably *shrinks* the ignition surface. But that is an argument, not a measurement: the wall must sit at the conversion door (two-atom credit, chart gate if needed), the bars must be the measured ones (bath byte-stats, glow/birth rates), staged per grok, with the bath armed. Note also that "nothing ever condenses" is no longer the right bar — QUENCH measured lawful creation; the bar is *bath statistics unchanged at inert, creation rate matching the measured quench law when live*.
- **Retention:** complexification gives phase a protected place to live (slot-borne, per D-3 — the QUENCH-3 lesson that the naked cell clock loses phase at 0.2 rad/t.u.), but the door is where retention is decided, and the door change is the genuinely new, risky piece: **the fired atom must carry arg(ψ) — departure phase from the composed slot amplitude, not m·th2** (grok's criterion 1). The field's own record proves the pattern can work: single-quantum clicks rebuild fringes, so conversion there is phase-faithful statistically. The winding-retention bar (already in the README) is the decisive test; the torus framing tells you it's a phase-slip rate you're measuring.

**Ranking.** Build it as: **complex parcel + DEC-form currents** (the implementation — J on links, cells update by δJ, doors as explicit source terms, unitary hops as the conservation mechanism), **torus/winding as the acceptance programme** (slip rates at doors, winding retention, ring circulation quantization), **uptake/outtake as the interpretation of J's moments and of the doors** (monopole = metabolism, dipole = momentum, intake/outtake = the two mode boundaries). **Projective: drop it** — it renames state you already store.
