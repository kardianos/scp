## 4. The mover audit protocol (whichever menu option is chosen)

1. Write the candidate table `laws/laws_V3<x>.cfg` (adopted values
   become defaults; knobs remain, now meaning "deviate from law").
2. Full battery at the new table; list EVERY bar that moves with
   old/new values.
3. With the user, per bar: SHARPEN (new value is the better claim),
   RE-GAUGE (bar re-pinned to the new law consciously), or REJECT
   (the move is a regression — adoption fails or the candidate is
   amended). No silent softening.
4. Battery pins and both kernels' header/purity lines updated; the
   inert-default checks re-pointed at the new table; C≡Go
   re-verified; commit is the adoption event.
