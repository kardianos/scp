# The principle

The foundational statement. Everything else in v88 is subordinate to this, and
anything that contradicts it is wrong regardless of how well it computes.

---

## 1. Conservation, stated the useful way

**Energy is never destroyed.**

At large scales, and in algebra, conservation is usually stated as *the books
balance* — totals agree before and after. That framing is true but weak here,
because it permits reasoning in which something vanishes at one place and
appears at another and the accounting is done afterwards.

Sub-quantum, the useful statement is stronger and local: **energy is never
destroyed.** It is never removed, never created, never transported as an
abstraction. It only ever *changes mode*. Every event is a conversion.

This is not an additional law layered on top of a dynamics. **It is the only
law.** There is no separate equation of motion for conservation to constrain.

---

## 2. Modes

Energy exists in modes. The modes include:

| mode | what it is |
|---|---|
| **space** | space itself is a form of energy, not a container that holds it |
| **dense pattern** | mass — fabric patterning |
| **field pattern** | EMF — fabric patterning |

Mass and EMF are both *patternings of the fabric*; they differ in the pattern,
not in the substance. Space is not a different kind of thing from them — it is
the same energy in a different mode.

Other modes are not excluded. The list is what is established, not a closure.

---

## 3. Conversion

Energy changes mode by **harmonic coupling of chiral planes**.

A link between regions actuates in the two planes transverse to it, with a
relative phase that is its handedness. Coupling through those chiral planes is
the mechanism by which one mode becomes another. Conversion proceeds through
complete cycles; a partial cycle converts nothing.

---

## 4. What follows

### 4.1 There is no background

Space is a **mode**, not a stage. There is therefore nothing for anything to
move through, sit in, or be located against. Any model with a permanent index
set — coordinates that persist while contents change — has smuggled a background
in, whatever its prose says.

### 4.2 Where there is matter, there is less space

Matter *is* converted space. Not matter placed in space, not matter displacing
space: the energy that is now pattern was previously space, and is no longer.
The amount of space is therefore dynamical, not fixed.

### 4.3 Space curves, and it curves **because** energy is never destroyed

This is the consequence, and it is a derivation rather than an addition:

Converting space into matter at a place removes space *there*. Because energy is
never destroyed, that removal cannot simply be a gap — the remaining space must
account for it. The space around a mass is therefore distorted, and the
distortion is not a field imposed on space; it is what conservation looks like
when space is one of the modes.

**Gravity is not a force and not a separate interaction. It is conservation of
energy, expressed geometrically, because space is convertible.**

### 4.4 Motion is regular conversion

Nothing persists and moves. A structure ceases and a structure comes to be, and
the regularity of that succession is what is observed as motion. Ahead of a
moving particle, space converts to pattern; behind it, pattern converts back to
space. Nothing traverses anything.

Identity across time is a reconstruction, not a fact. "Where is it now" has no
fundamental answer — only "what is the pattern of conversion."

### 4.5 c is the conversion rate

The speed limit is not a limit on travel, because nothing travels. It is the
maximum rate at which chiral harmonic coupling can complete a cycle and carry a
conversion forward. **c is derived, not imposed.**

## 5. What this retires

* **Any model with immortal coordinates.** A fixed lattice with evolving
  contents is a background. This includes every instrument built in v88 so far.
* **Profiles.** A shape `f(r)` is a shape *in* something. Q-balls are void from
  the first line — not because they lack a discrete spectrum (a symptom) but
  because they are defined as persistent things occupying places.
* **Trajectories, and equations of motion in the usual sense.** There is no
  quantity whose position evolves.
* **Momentum as a fundamental conserved quantity.** Momentum references a
  background. Where there is none, there is nothing for it to be conserved
  against.
* **A fixed volume budget.** The earlier constraint `Σᵢθᵢ = 0` treated total
  space as pinned and merely redistributable. It is not: space converts to
  pattern. What is pinned is total *energy across all modes*, not total volume.

## 6. Retrodiction: this was already measured

In this project's own runs, on a discrete fabric:

| quantity | conservation measured |
|---|---|
| charge (per component) | **exact to 13 significant figures** — `50.846808956130` unchanged |
| momentum | ~10⁻⁴, integrator quality only |

The asymmetry was recorded as a defect of the discretisation. It is not. **Charge
is a property of structure and does not reference position, so it survives
structures being destroyed and created. Momentum references a background, and
there is none.** The measurement anticipated the principle.

Likewise the discrete-momentum derivation found that translation is broken to a
discrete subgroup, so no Noether momentum current exists. That was written up as
a structural defect bounding what could be achieved. It is instead a direct
observation that there is no background to translate through.

## 7. Open

* **What sets the direction and rate of conversion.** Conservation says energy
  changes mode; it does not by itself say which way a given transformation runs,
  or when. Presumably a condition on the chiral coupling — conversion proceeds
  where cycles can close and stalls where they cannot — but this is not
  established.
* **The full mode list.** Space, dense pattern, field pattern are established.
  Whether these are complete, and what distinguishes the patternings of mass
  from those of EMF, is not.
* **The regular method.** Which successions of structure are permitted. This is
  the content that replaces equations of motion, and it does not yet exist.
* **Related prior work.** Quantum Ring Theory (Guglinski) is noted as the
  closest developed theory in concept. Not yet examined here; the resemblance is
  reported, not assessed.
